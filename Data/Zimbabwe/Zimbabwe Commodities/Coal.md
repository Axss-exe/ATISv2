## Coal

**Category:** 
**Chemical Formula:** C (variable, ~60–90% carbon)
**Global Royalty Range:** 2–10%

## Summary

A combustible sedimentary rock of carbonized plant matter. Formed from peat through burial, heat, and pressure over millions of years. Ranked by carbon content: lignite → sub-bituminous → bituminous → anthracite.


## Geological Occurrence

- 1. Sedimentary basins — Karoo (Southern Africa), Permian (Australia), Carboniferous (USA, Europe, China)
2. Deltaic deposits — Powder River Basin, Indonesia
3. Synclinal basins — Ruhr, Donbas
4. Tertiary lignites — Germany, Poland, India
5. Gondwana basins — India, South America, Africa


## Major End Products

- 1. Thermal power generation (steam coal)
2. Steelmaking coke & metallurgical heat
3. Cement manufacturing (clinker kilns)
4. Synthetic liquid fuels (CTL — coal-to-liquids)
5. Chemical feedstock (ammonia, methanol, activated carbon)


## Standard Processing Workflow

- 1. Open-pit or underground extraction
2. Run-of-mine (ROM) crushing & screening
3. Dense medium separation (washing/beneficiation) to reduce ash & sulphur
4. Sizing (steam coal vs. metallurgical fractions)
5. Coking (metallurgical coal only): heating in absence of oxygen to produce coke
6. Pulverization (thermal coal for power stations)
7. Briquetting or pelletizing (domestic/industrial use)


## Common Mining Methods

- • Open-pit dragline / strip mining (shallow seams)
• Underground longwall mining (deep, flat seams)
• Room and pillar (steep or thin seams)
• Highwall mining (seam extensions)
• Mountaintop removal (Appalachia)
• Continuous miner (underground mechanized)
