## Gold

**Category:** 
**Chemical Formula:** Au
**Global Royalty Range:** 1–7%

## Summary

A dense, soft, yellow precious metal. Highly ductile and malleable. Excellent conductor of electricity. Occurs native and in compounds.


## Geological Occurrence

- 1. Archaean greenstone belts — Zimbabwe, Ghana, Tanzania, Canada, Australia
2. Orogenic shear zones — Abitibi, Yilgarn, Ashanti
3. Epithermal deposits — Nevada, Papua New Guinea, Indonesia
4. Alluvial / placer deposits — river systems globally
5. Porphyry copper-gold — Chile, Indonesia, Mongolia
6. Carlin-type sedimentary — Nevada


## Major End Products

- 1. Investment bullion & sovereign coins
2. Fine jewelry (22K, 18K, 14K alloys)
3. Electronics connectors, contacts & bonding wire
4. Dental & medical alloys (crowns, implants)
5. Aerospace radiation shielding & thermal control


## Standard Processing Workflow

- 1. Mining (hard rock or alluvial)
2. Crushing & grinding
3. Gravity concentration (shaking tables, centrifuges, spirals)
4. Cyanide leaching (CIP / CIL circuits)
5. Carbon stripping (elution)
6. Electrowinning or zinc precipitation (Merrill-Crowe)
7. Smelting to doré bar (85–92% Au)
8. Electrolytic refining to 99.99% (London Good Delivery)


## Common Mining Methods

- • Open-pit (low-grade bulk deposits)
• Underground shaft/decline (vein deposits)
• Longhole stoping
• Cut and fill
• Alluvial dredging & sluicing
• Artisanal & small-scale mining (panning, sluicing, cyanidation)
