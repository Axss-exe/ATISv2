## Rare Earths

**Category:** 
**Chemical Formula:** Variable (REE suite: La, Ce, Pr, Nd, Sm, Eu, Gd, Tb, Dy, Ho, Er, Tm, Yb, Lu, Sc, Y)
**Global Royalty Range:** 2–8%

## Summary

A group of 17 chemically similar elements. Not rare in Earth's crust but rarely concentrated in mineable deposits. Divided into Light REE (La–Sm) and Heavy REE (Eu–Lu + Y). Critical for high-tech applications.


## Geological Occurrence

- 1. Carbonatite intrusions — Mountain Pass (USA), Bayan Obo (China), Kangankunde (Malawi), Dorowa (Zimbabwe)
2. Alkaline igneous complexes — Lovozero (Russia), Sørensen (Greenland)
3. Ion-adsorption clays — Southern China (unique weathered granites)
4. Placer / monazite sands — India, Brazil, Australia (beach sands)
5. Apatite-iron ores — Kiruna-type (by-product REE)


## Major End Products

- 1. High-strength permanent magnets (NdFeB) for EVs & wind turbines
2. EV traction motors, generators & magnetic drives
3. Wind turbine direct-drive generators (especially offshore)
4. Smartphone screens, speakers, vibration motors & camera lenses
5. Precision-guided missile systems, radar & night-vision optics


## Standard Processing Workflow

- 1. Open-pit mining of carbonatite / alkaline ore
2. Crushing & grinding
3. Flotation or acid baking (H₂SO₄ roast at 300–500°C)
4. Acid leaching (H₂SO₄ or HCl) to dissolve REEs into solution
5. Solvent extraction (SX) cascade — 100+ stages to separate individual REEs
6. Precipitation as oxalates or carbonates
7. Calcination to REE oxides (Nd₂O₃, Pr₆O₁₁, Dy₂O₃, etc.)
8. Electrolytic reduction to metals (for magnet alloys)
9. Alloy production (NdFeB, SmCo)


## Common Mining Methods

- • Open-pit (carbonatite deposits — Mountain Pass, Bayan Obo)
• Underground (for deeper alkaline complexes)
• In-situ leaching (ion-adsorption clays — Southern China only)
• Dredging (monazite beach sands — India, Brazil)
• Not yet mined at scale in Zimbabwe
