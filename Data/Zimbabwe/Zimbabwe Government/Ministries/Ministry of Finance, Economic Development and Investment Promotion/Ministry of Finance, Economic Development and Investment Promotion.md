#### Function
The Ministry of Finance, Economic Development and Investment Promotion is mandated to formulate and implement fiscal, economic and investment policies that foster sustainable growth, fiscal stability, and equitable development for the nation. The Ministry oversees the preparation of the national budget, monitors public expenditures, and manages state revenue collection, ensuring transparency and accountability in fiscal operations. It coordinates macroeconomic planning, conducts economic analysis, and provides policy advice to support diversification, productivity enhancement, and job creation. The Ministry regulates and promotes domestic and foreign investment by streamlining procedures, offering incentives, and maintaining an investment climate conducive to private sector participation. It administers public debt, negotiates financing arrangements with international partners, and monitors debt sustainability. The Ministry collaborates with other government agencies, multilateral institutions, and the private sector to align development projects with national priorities, facilitate public-private partnerships, and support infrastructure financing.
