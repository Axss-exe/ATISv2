#### Function
The Ministry of Defence is mandated to safeguard national security by providing strategic direction, operational readiness, and sustainable capabilities for the armed forces. The Ministry formulates defence policy, allocates resources, and oversees the procurement, development, and maintenance of military equipment and infrastructure. It coordinates the training, deployment, and support of the Army, Air Force and other defence services, ensuring that personnel are prepared for a range of contingencies, from conventional warfare to humanitarian assistance. The Ministry manages intelligence and cyber defence functions, collaborates with allied nations and international organisations, and contributes to peacekeeping and stability operations abroad. It supervises defence research and innovation programmes, promotes interoperability across services, and monitors compliance with legal and ethical standards. The Ministry provides strategic advice to the government, conducts risk assessments, and engages with the public and stakeholders to maintain transparency and accountability.
