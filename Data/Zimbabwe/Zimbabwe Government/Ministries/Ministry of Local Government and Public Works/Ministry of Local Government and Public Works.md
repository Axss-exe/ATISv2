#### Function
The Ministry of Local Government and Public Works aims to promote socioeconomic development of rural and urban communities in a well-built devolved environment. The Ministry administers a decentralized system of urban and rural district councils, ward and village assemblies, and provincial councils to facilitate local decision-making and resource sharing. Its core operations are organised into several departments: Urban Development, Rural Development, Public Works, Local Government, Traditional Leadership Welfare, Provincial Affairs and Devolution, and Internal Administration. The Ministry oversees the Liquor Licensing Board, which processes and issues liquor licences under the Liquor Act. Ongoing projects include market openings, partnerships with municipal bodies, and initiatives such as the Zunde RaMambo food aid programme. All activities are guided by the vision of devolved governance for prosperous communities by 2030.
