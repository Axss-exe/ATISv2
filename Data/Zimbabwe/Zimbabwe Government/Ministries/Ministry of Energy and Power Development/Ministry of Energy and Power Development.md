#### Function
The Ministry of Energy and Power Development is mandated to ensure energy supply and security for the entire nation while promoting the development and efficient utilization of renewable and non-renewable energy sources. The Ministry delivers its mandate through policy formulation, regulatory oversight, and the implementation of infrastructure projects across thermal, hydro, biogas, and rural electrification sectors. It manages national electricity generation capacity, monitors fuel consumption, and coordinates the Rural Electrification Fund and other strategic planning initiatives. The Ministry oversees power sector reforms, facilitates investment in energy infrastructure, and promotes energy conservation and efficiency. It collaborates with the Zimbabwe Energy Regulatory Authority and parliamentary committees to align regulatory frameworks with national energy objectives.
