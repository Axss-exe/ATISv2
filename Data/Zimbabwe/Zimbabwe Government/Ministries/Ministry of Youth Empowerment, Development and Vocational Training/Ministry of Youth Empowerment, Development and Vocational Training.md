#### Function
The Ministry of Youth Empowerment, Development and Vocational Training is mandated to empower Zimbabwe's youth by providing leadership, entrepreneurial, vocational and technical opportunities that foster personal development, economic participation and national contribution. The Ministry operates through core departments delivering policies, funding, incubation and public-private partnerships that support youth entrepreneurship, skills acquisition and civic engagement. Key programmes include vocational training initiatives that combine technical curricula with industry linkages, and project-based interventions targeting specific sectors such as agriculture, technology, and aquaculture. The Ministry promotes national identity and civic responsibility through career guidance services and placement programmes. Its core values guide the delivery of services aimed at creating sustainable livelihoods and fostering inclusive economic growth for Zimbabwe's young population.
