#### Function
The Ministry of Higher and Tertiary Education, Innovation, Science and Technology Development is mandated to advance national socioeconomic progress by fostering accessible, quality higher education and by promoting research, innovation, and technology development that respond to the country's development priorities. The Ministry formulates and implements policies governing universities, colleges, vocational institutes, and research centres, ensuring alignment with national strategic plans. It oversees accreditation and quality assurance mechanisms, monitors institutional performance, and coordinates the allocation of public funding for teaching, research, and infrastructure projects. The Ministry administers scholarship and grant programmes that support student mobility, postgraduate studies, and collaborative research initiatives. It facilitates partnerships between academia, industry, and international bodies to stimulate knowledge transfer, entrepreneurship, and the commercialization of scientific outcomes. The Ministry provides regulatory oversight for emerging technology sectors, supports capacity building programmes for faculty and staff, and maintains statistical databases to inform evidence-based decision-making.
