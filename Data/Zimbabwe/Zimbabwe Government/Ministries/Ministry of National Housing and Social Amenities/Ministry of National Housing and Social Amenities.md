#### Function
The Ministry of National Housing and Social Amenities aims to lead in the provision of human settlements in a coordinated and sustainable manner through the facilitation and promotion of functional housing and social amenities for all Zimbabweans. The Ministry delivers a range of operational services, including advisory support for walk-in clients and written communication, issuance of letters of support for prescribed asset status, and facilitation of civil service housing loans. It provides stock-type model building plans, processes applications for housing accommodation, and manages the design, construction, and maintenance of government housing estates. Additional functions comprise the issuance of title deeds for surveyed and fully paid properties, processing of cessions, lease renewals for government-enabled accommodation, and adjudication of rent and eviction disputes. The Ministry oversees project design and management, and maintains a portfolio of ongoing housing projects across provinces. Recent initiatives include a multi-pronged housing finance strategy to improve access for developers and citizens, and participation in regional and international forums such as UN-Habitat and the World Urban Forum.
