#### Function
The Ministry of Mines and Mining Development is mandated to promote sustainable investment, exploration, mining, value addition, beneficiation, marketing and management of Zimbabwe's mineral resources for the benefit of all Zimbabweans. The Ministry administers the full mining value chain, issuing and renewing mining titles, special grants and investment licences, and overseeing compliance with land use and environmental regulations. It coordinates the sale of gold through Fidelity Gold Refiners and facilitates market access for other minerals via the Minerals Marketing Corporation of Zimbabwe. The Ministry manages the registration of mining companies, monitors active mines, and publishes statistics on mineral potential, active operations and investment opportunities. The Ministry disseminates information through its website, press statements, and outreach events such as provincial meetings, the Chamber of Mines conferences and the Zimbabwe International Trade Fair. Feedback and inquiries are directed to the Secretary for Mines and Mining Development at its Harare office.
