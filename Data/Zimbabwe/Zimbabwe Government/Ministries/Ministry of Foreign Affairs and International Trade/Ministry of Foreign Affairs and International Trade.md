#### Function
The Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Trade is mandated to promote the political and economic interests, image and influence of the Republic of Zimbabwe in the international community and to protect the interests and safety of Zimbabwean nationals abroad. The Ministry implements Zimbabwe's foreign policy as set by the President and pursues objectives that include safeguarding sovereignty, enhancing the nation's prestige, advancing social justice oriented policies, and fostering an international environment conducive to peace, security, and development. Its core operations encompass diplomatic engagement, maintaining communication with Zimbabwean missions abroad and foreign missions in Harare, and conducting negotiations on political, economic, social and cultural agreements. The Ministry supports trade and investment through sectoral outreach and positions Zimbabwe as a leading African economy for global investment. It provides consular and legal services, including passport issuance, police clearances, and assistance to Zimbabwean nationals. The Ministry ensures compliance with regional and continental treaty obligations, and contributes to capacity building workshops and summit activities. It disseminates news, policy statements, and information on foreign policy priorities via its website and media centre.
