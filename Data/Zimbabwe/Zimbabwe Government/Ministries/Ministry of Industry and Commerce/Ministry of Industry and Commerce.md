#### Function
The Ministry of Industry and Commerce is mandated to accelerate inclusive industrialisation and commercialisation programmes that foster employment creation, economic empowerment and structural transformation across the country. Operating under the National Development Strategy, the Ministry implements programmes and services aimed at strengthening the manufacturing and commercial sectors. These include the Local Content Strategy for rural industrialisation, value chain mapping to promote the use of local raw materials, and business development programmes. The Ministry manages import-export licensing, standards development, and the administration of the Rent Board and Reserved Sectors. It provides a complaints handling mechanism, publishes policies and strategies, and maintains a head office in Harare for public inquiries.
