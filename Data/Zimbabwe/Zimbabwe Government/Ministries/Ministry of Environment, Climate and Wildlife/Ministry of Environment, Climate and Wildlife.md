#### Function
The Ministry of Environment, Climate and Wildlife is tasked with safeguarding natural resources, mitigating climate change impacts, and preserving biodiversity to ensure sustainable development and the well-being of present and future generations. The Ministry formulates and implements national environmental policies, aligning them with international conventions on climate action, wildlife protection, and ecosystem management. It conducts environmental impact assessments for public and private projects, issuing permits and monitoring compliance with standards on air quality, water resources, waste management, and emissions. The Ministry oversees the establishment and management of protected areas, wildlife reserves, and marine parks, coordinating anti-poaching initiatives and species recovery programs. It administers climate adaptation strategies, including the development of greenhouse gas inventories, promotion of renewable energy incentives, and support for climate-resilient agriculture. The Ministry collaborates with other government agencies, research institutions, NGOs, and local communities to facilitate capacity building, public awareness campaigns, and stakeholder participation in environmental decision-making. It manages funding mechanisms, such as environmental grants and climate finance instruments, to support conservation projects, sustainable land use planning, and the transition to low-carbon economies.
