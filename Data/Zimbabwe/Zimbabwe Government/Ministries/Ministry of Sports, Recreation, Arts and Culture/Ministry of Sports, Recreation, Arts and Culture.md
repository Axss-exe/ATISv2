#### Function
The Ministry of Sports, Recreation, Arts and Culture is mandated to foster the development and promotion of sports, recreation, arts and cultural affairs in Zimbabwe. The Ministry formulates and implements policies and strategies that ensure the development of sport, cultural and creative industries, and recreation. It establishes and administers revolving funds to stimulate the growth of sports, arts and recreation industries. The Ministry capacitates stakeholders through skills training, strengthens sports and recreation associations, and promotes entrepreneurial skills development. It oversees the Sports and Recreation Commission, National Arts Council, National Gallery, and National Museums and Monuments. The Ministry promotes national identity and civic responsibility through cultural expressions, and ensures equitable participation and sustainable development in line with national Vision 2030.
