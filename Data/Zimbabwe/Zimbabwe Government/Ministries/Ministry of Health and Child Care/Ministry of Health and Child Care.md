#### Function
The Ministry of Health and Child Care is mandated to protect, promote, and improve the health and well-being of the nation's population, with a particular focus on children, through the delivery of comprehensive health services, policy development, and coordinated public health initiatives. The Ministry's operations span curative services including hospitals, pharmacy, nursing, oral health, pathology, laboratory, traditional medicine, rehabilitation, and mental health care, and public health functions including HIV/AIDS and TB control, epidemiology, disease surveillance, health promotion, malaria control, environmental health, radiation protection, non-communicable disease programmes, and nutrition. The Ministry oversees a broad administrative structure that includes finance, human resource management, procurement, legal services, ICT, and quality assurance units, as well as a National Institute of Health Research and a Health Informatics and Data Analytics unit. The Zimbabwe Health Information and Support Project has strengthened the national health information system by deploying DHIS-2 across districts, provinces, and the central office, and introduced a mobile-phone-based Weekly Disease Surveillance System using FrontlineSMS, enabling real-time reporting of disease events and health indicators. These tools have raised data completeness and timeliness, supporting evidence-based decision-making, outbreak detection, and improved service delivery throughout the country.
