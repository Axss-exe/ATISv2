#### Function
The Ministry of Transport and Infrastructural Development is mandated to formulate, implement, and oversee policies that ensure safe, efficient, and sustainable transport systems and infrastructure that support national economic growth and social welfare. The Ministry coordinates the planning, construction, and maintenance of road networks, railways, ports, airports, and urban transit facilities, aligning projects with national development plans and international standards. It regulates transport operators, issues licenses, and monitors compliance with safety, environmental, and service quality regulations across all modes of transport. The Ministry manages the allocation of public and private investment, conducts feasibility studies, and supervises tender processes for major infrastructure projects. It collaborates with other government agencies, local authorities, and private sector partners to integrate multimodal transport solutions, promote digitalization, and enhance logistics efficiency. The Ministry monitors performance indicators, prepares statistical reports, and provides technical guidance to improve infrastructure resilience, reduce congestion, and support the mobility needs of citizens and businesses.
