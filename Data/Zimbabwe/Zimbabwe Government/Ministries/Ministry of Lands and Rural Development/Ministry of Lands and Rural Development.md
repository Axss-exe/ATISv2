#### Function
The Ministry of Lands and Rural Development is mandated to promote equitable land administration, sustainable rural development, and inclusive socioeconomic growth by formulating policies, delivering services, and coordinating stakeholders across the nation. The Ministry oversees land registration, titling, and cadastral mapping, ensuring legal certainty and facilitating investment. It administers land use planning, zoning, and allocation of public and private lands for agriculture, housing, infrastructure, and conservation, while enforcing compliance with national land laws. The Ministry implements rural development programmes that improve livelihoods through agricultural extension, irrigation projects, rural infrastructure upgrades, and capacity building initiatives for smallholder farmers and community groups. It coordinates with other government agencies, local authorities, and development partners to align strategies, share data, and mobilize resources. Monitoring and evaluation units track the impact of land and rural policies, producing periodic reports that inform policy revisions. The Ministry provides public information services, grievance redress mechanisms, and training for land officers to enhance transparency and accountability in land governance and rural development processes.
