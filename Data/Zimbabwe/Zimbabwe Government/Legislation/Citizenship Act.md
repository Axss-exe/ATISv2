# Citizenship Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Home Affairs and Cultural Heritage]]"
  - "[[Registrar General's Office]]"


## Description
This law governs the acquisition, loss, and restoration of Zimbabwean citizenship. It was created to define nationality, regulate citizenship rights, and manage the legal status of residents. It regulates all sectors through citizenship status affecting property ownership, business rights, and political participation. Investors should care because it determines eligibility for naturalization, dual citizenship provisions, and the rights of non-citizens to own property, vote, and hold certain business licenses, intersecting with immigration and land ownership laws.
