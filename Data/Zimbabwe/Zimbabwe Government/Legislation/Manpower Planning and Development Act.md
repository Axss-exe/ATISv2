# Manpower Planning and Development Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Higher and Tertiary Education, Innovation, Science and Technology Development]]"
  - "[[National Manpower Advisory Council]]"


## Description
This law provides for the establishment and operation of technical and vocational institutions, universities, and teachers colleges. It was created to address skills shortages, finance workforce development through employer levies, and build a skilled national workforce. It regulates technical education, vocational training, manufacturing, construction, and mining sectors. Investors should care because it imposes training levies on employers to finance skills development, governs apprenticeship training, trade testing, and the certification of skilled workers, directly impacting corporate training obligations and workforce development costs.
