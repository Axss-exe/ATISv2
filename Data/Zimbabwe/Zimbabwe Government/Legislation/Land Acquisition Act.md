# Land Acquisition Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Lands, Agriculture, Water, Climate and Rural Resettlement]]"
  - "[[Department of Lands]]"


## Description
This law provides the legal framework for the compulsory acquisition of land by the State for public purposes, including resettlement and agricultural reform. It was created to enable land reform and redistribution. It regulates agriculture, real estate, mining, and urban development sectors. Investors should care because it outlines compensation procedures, valuation methodologies, and appeal mechanisms, with significant implications for land-based investments and property rights security.
