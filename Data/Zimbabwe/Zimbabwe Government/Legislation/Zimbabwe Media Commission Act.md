# Zimbabwe Media Commission Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Zimbabwe Media Commission (ZMC)]]"
  - "[[Ministry of Information, Publicity and Broadcasting Services]]"


## Description
This law, enacted in April 2021, establishes the Zimbabwe Media Commission and elaborates on its constitutional functions under Section 248 and 249. It was created to regulate the media industry, uphold freedom of the media, enforce ethical standards, and promote media diversity and fair competition. It regulates media, broadcasting, journalism, online publishing, and digital communications sectors. Investors should care because it defines media broadly to include internet and electronic dissemination, regulates media practitioners beyond traditional journalists, establishes the Media Fund for capacity building, and provides complaint and investigation procedures for media violations, affecting all entities engaged in content creation, publishing, or digital media operations.
