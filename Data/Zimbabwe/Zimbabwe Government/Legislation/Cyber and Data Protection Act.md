# Cyber and Data Protection Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Postal and Telecommunications Regulatory Authority of Zimbabwe (POTRAZ)]]"
  - "[[Cyber Security and Monitoring Centre]]"


## Description
This law is Zimbabwe's principal data protection and cybersecurity legislation, enacted as Chapter 12:07 in 2021. It was created to increase cybersecurity, build confidence in the secure use of information and communication technologies, and protect personal data in line with constitutional rights. It regulates all sectors that process personal data, including banking, telecommunications, e-commerce, healthcare, and government services. Investors should care because it mandates registration and licensing of data controllers, appointment of data protection officers for licensed controllers, breach notification within 24 hours to POTRAZ, and cross-border data transfer restrictions, with penalties including fines up to Level 11 and imprisonment up to 7 years.
