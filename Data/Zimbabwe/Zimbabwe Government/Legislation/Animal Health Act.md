# Animal Health Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Lands, Agriculture, Water, Climate and Rural Resettlement]]"
  - "[[Department of Veterinary Services]]"


## Description
This law governs the control of animal diseases and the regulation of animal imports. It was created to prevent the spread of animal diseases, protect the livestock industry, and ensure food safety and public health. It regulates livestock, dairy, poultry, veterinary pharmaceuticals, import, and export sectors. Investors should care because it requires import permits for live animals, animal products, and infectious things, establishes veterinary inspection powers, quarantine facilities, and disease control measures including compulsory slaughter and compensation, essential for livestock and veterinary pharmaceutical investors.
