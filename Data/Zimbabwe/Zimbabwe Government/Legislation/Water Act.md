# Water Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Lands, Agriculture, Water, Climate and Rural Resettlement]]"
  - "[[Zimbabwe National Water Authority (ZINWA)]]"


## Description
This law provides for the planning, development, and utilization of water resources. It was created to ensure sustainable water use, equitable distribution, and the establishment of catchment-based management. It regulates agriculture, mining, manufacturing, and urban water supply sectors. Investors should care because it vests all water in the President, establishes water rights and permits for abstraction, and creates the framework for catchment councils and water user boards, which is critical for irrigation, mining, and industrial water use licensing.
