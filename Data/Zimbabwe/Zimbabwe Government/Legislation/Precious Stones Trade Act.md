# Precious Stones Trade Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[ATIS/Zimbabwe/Zimbabwe Government/Ministries/Ministry of Mines and Mining Development]], [[Zimbabwe Republic Police (ZRP)]]"


## Description
This law restricts and regulates the commercial possession, sorting, and export tracking of precious stones. It was created to prevent illicit diamond trading, ensure state control over precious stone exports, and combat smuggling. It regulates mining, precious stones, diamond trade, and jewelry sectors. Investors should care because it imposes severe criminal penalties for unauthorized possession or dealing in diamonds and emeralds, and mandates strict export tracking procedures.
