# Mines and Minerals Act

**Status**: Active - Under Revision
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Mines and Mining Development]]"


## Description
This law is the foundational legislation for extractive operations in Zimbabwe. It was created to vest all mineral rights in the President and regulate mining activities to ensure orderly exploitation of mineral resources. It regulates mining, exploration, and mineral processing sectors. Investors should care because it sets out the processes for acquiring prospecting licenses, Exclusive Prospecting Orders (EPOs), and mining leases, which are prerequisites for any mineral extraction venture, and is currently under revision.
