# Standards Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Standards Association of Zimbabwe (SAZ)]]"
  - "[[Ministry of Industry and Commerce]]"


## Description
This law provides for the establishment of the Standards Development Fund and the regulation of product standards, metrology, and quality certification. It was created to ensure product quality, consumer safety, and industrial competitiveness in domestic and export markets. It regulates manufacturing, trade, industry, and product development sectors. Investors should care because it mandates compliance with Zimbabwean standards (ZWS) for manufactured goods, imports, and industrial processes, affecting manufacturing quality, market access, and trade competitiveness.
