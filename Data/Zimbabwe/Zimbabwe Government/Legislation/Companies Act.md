# Companies Act

**Status**: Repealed
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Registrar of Companies]]"


## Description
This law was the historic primary act governing corporate registries and company setup in Zimbabwe. It was created to provide a legal framework for company formation, corporate governance, and shareholder rights. It regulated all sectors. Investors should care because it has been completely replaced by the modern Companies and Other Business Entities Act (COBE Act) and is now only relevant for legacy joint-venture query tracking and historical corporate records.
