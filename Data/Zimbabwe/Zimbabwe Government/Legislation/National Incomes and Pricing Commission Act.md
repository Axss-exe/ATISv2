# National Incomes and Pricing Commission Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[National Incomes and Pricing Commission (NIPC)]]"
  - "[[Ministry of Industry and Commerce]]"


## Description
This law establishes the National Incomes and Pricing Commission to regulate prices, incomes, and monopolies. It was created to prevent price gouging, ensure fair pricing, protect consumers from exploitation, and investigate monopolistic practices. It regulates manufacturing, retail, import, export, and consumer goods sectors. Investors should care because it provides for price controls on essential commodities, anti-monopoly measures, and the investigation of unfair pricing practices, remaining the statutory basis for government price monitoring and affecting pricing strategies for imported and locally manufactured goods.
