# Anti-Corruption Commission Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Zimbabwe Anti-Corruption Commission (ZACC)]]"
  - "[[Minister of Special Affairs in the President's Office]]"


## Description
This law establishes the Zimbabwe Anti-Corruption Commission (ZACC) as an independent constitutional commission. It was created to investigate and combat corruption in public and private sectors, promote transparency, and uphold the rule of law. It regulates public sector, private sector, government contracting, mining, and finance sectors. Investors should care because it provides for the investigation of corruption complaints involving public officers and private entities, defines offences related to corruption, and mandates cooperation with law enforcement and prosecuting authorities, underpinning corporate governance and integrity compliance.
