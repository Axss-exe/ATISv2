# Banking Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe (RBZ)]]"


## Description
This law provides for the licensing, regulation, and supervision of banking institutions. It was created to ensure financial sector stability, protect depositors, and maintain confidence in the banking system. It regulates banking, finance, investment, and corporate services sectors. Investors should care because it establishes prudential requirements, capital adequacy ratios, corporate governance standards for banks, and depositor protection mechanisms, forming the foundational law for the financial sector and foreign bank branch operations.
