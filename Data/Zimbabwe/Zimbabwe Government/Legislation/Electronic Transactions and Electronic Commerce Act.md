# Electronic Transactions and Electronic Commerce Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Information Communication Technology, Postal and Courier Services]]"
  - "[[Postal and Telecommunications Regulatory Authority (POTRAZ)]]"


## Description
This law, Chapter 8:20 enacted in 2022, provides the legal framework for electronic signatures, digital contracts, and electronic commerce. It was created to enable digital transformation, e-governance, and paperless business processes by harmonizing fragmented older laws and aligning with the UNCITRAL Model Law on Electronic Commerce. It regulates e-commerce, fintech, banking, telecommunications, digital services, and online business sectors. Investors should care because it legally recognizes electronic signatures (including advanced electronic signatures requiring accredited certification authorities), makes electronic documents admissible in court, and enables digital contracting, procurement, and service agreements, forming the backbone for online business operations.
