# Official Secrets Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Defense]]"
  - "[[Ministry of Home Affairs and Cultural Heritage]]"
  - "[[Zimbabwe Republic Police (ZRP)]]"


## Description
This law regulates the protection of state secrets, classified information, and official documents from unauthorized disclosure. It was created to safeguard national security, prevent espionage, and ensure confidentiality of sensitive government information. It regulates government administration, defense, intelligence, telecommunications, data handling, and any business contracting with government on sensitive matters. Investors should care because it criminalizes the unauthorized disclosure, possession, or communication of official secrets, imposes strict liability for breaches, and affects any business handling classified government information, defense contracts, or sensitive data, requiring robust information security protocols and clearance procedures.
