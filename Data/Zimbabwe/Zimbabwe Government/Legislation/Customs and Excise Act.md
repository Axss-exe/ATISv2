# Customs and Excise Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Zimbabwe Revenue Authority (ZIMRA)]]"
  - "[[Ministry of Finance, Economic Development and Investment Promotion]]"


## Description
This law is the primary customs legislation governing the importation and exportation of goods. It was created to regulate cross-border trade, collect customs revenue, prevent smuggling, and protect local industry from unfair competition. It regulates import, export, manufacturing, trade, logistics, and mining sectors. Investors should care because it establishes ports of entry, customs barriers, and examination procedures, regulates the valuation of goods for duty purposes, tariff classifications, duty drawback schemes, and customs bonds, fundamentally affecting all import/export operations and cross-border supply chains.
