# MMCZ Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[ATIS/Zimbabwe/Zimbabwe Businesses/Companies/Regulatory Agencies/Minerals Marketing Corporation of Zimbabwe (MMCZ)]]"


## Description
This law establishes the Minerals Marketing Corporation of Zimbabwe (MMCZ) as the sole marketing and selling agent for minerals produced in Zimbabwe. It was created to centralize mineral marketing, ensure state oversight of mineral exports, and maximize revenue from the extractive sector. It regulates mining, mineral trading, and export sectors. Investors should care because all covered mineral export contracts (excluding gold and silver) must go through the MMCZ, adding statutory commission layers to the export pipeline and affecting revenue realization timelines.
