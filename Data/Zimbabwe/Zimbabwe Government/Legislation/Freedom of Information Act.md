# Freedom of Information Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Zimbabwe Media Commission (ZMC)]]"
  - "[[Ministry of Information, Publicity and Broadcasting Services]]"


## Description
This law, Chapter 10:33 enacted in July 2020, provides for the right of access to information held by public and private entities. It was created to repeal the restrictive Access to Information and Protection of Privacy Act (AIPPA) and give effect to the constitutional right to access information under Section 61 and 62 of the Constitution. It regulates government transparency, media, journalism, research, corporate governance, and civil society sectors. Investors should care because it establishes procedures for requesting information from public and private entities, mandates information disclosure policies, and creates appeal mechanisms through the Zimbabwe Media Commission, promoting transparency and accountability in business and government dealings.
