# Broadcasting Services Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Zimbabwe Media Commission (ZMC)]]"
  - "[[Ministry of Information, Publicity and Broadcasting Services]]"


## Description
This law, Chapter 12:06, governs the licensing and regulation of broadcasting services and signal carriers. It was created to regulate the airwaves, ensure diversity of broadcasting content, and establish a framework for commercial, community, and subscription broadcasting services. It regulates broadcasting, media, telecommunications, internet streaming, and digital content sectors. Investors should care because it authorizes various classes of broadcasting services including commercial, community, subscription satellite, cable, datacasting, webcasting, and signal carrier operations, with strict licensing requirements and penalties including fines up to five million dollars and imprisonment up to two years for unlicensed broadcasting.
