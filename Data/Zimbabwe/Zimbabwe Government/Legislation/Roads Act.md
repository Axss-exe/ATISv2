# Roads Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Transport and Infrastructural Development]]"
  - "[[Department of Roads]]"
  - "[[District Development Fund (DDF)]]"


## Description
This law provides for the classification, construction, maintenance, and management of public roads. It was created to ensure safe and efficient road infrastructure, regulate road access, and facilitate transport and logistics. It regulates transport, logistics, construction, and infrastructure sectors. Investors should care because it establishes the framework for trunk roads, district roads, and urban roads, including powers to regulate road access, tolling, and road reserves, which is critical for logistics, distribution, and infrastructure development projects.
