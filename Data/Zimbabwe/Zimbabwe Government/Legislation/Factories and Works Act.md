# Factories and Works Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Public Service, Labour and Social Welfare]]"
  - "[[National Social Security Authority (NSSA)]]"


## Description
This law regulates occupational health and safety standards in industrial and manufacturing workplaces. It was created to protect workers from industrial hazards, ensure safe working environments, and prevent workplace accidents. It regulates manufacturing, industrial, mining, and construction sectors. Investors should care because it mandates factory registration, inspection of machinery, ventilation standards, and accident reporting, with NSSA enforcing complementary provisions through the Accident Prevention and Workers Compensation Scheme.
