# Partnerships Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Registrar of Companies]]"
  - "[[Ministry of Justice, Legal and Parliamentary Affairs]]"


## Description
This law governs the formation, operation, and dissolution of general partnerships and limited partnerships. It was created to provide a legal framework for business partnerships, clarify partner liability, and regulate partnership agreements. It regulates all sectors, particularly professional services, small business, and joint ventures. Investors should care because it is the primary framework for foreign investors entering Zimbabwe through partnership structures rather than incorporated entities, affecting joint venture governance, liability allocation, and profit-sharing arrangements.
