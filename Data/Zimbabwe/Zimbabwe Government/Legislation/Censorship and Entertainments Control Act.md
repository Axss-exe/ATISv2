# Censorship and Entertainments Control Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Home Affairs and Cultural Heritage]]"
  - "[[Board of Censors]]"


## Description
This law, Chapter 10:04, regulates the importation, production, and dissemination of publications, films, pictures, statues, and records deemed undesirable. It was created to protect public morals, prevent the circulation of harmful content, and regulate entertainment and media consumption. It regulates film, media, publishing, entertainment, digital content, and online streaming sectors. Investors should care because it prohibits the importation and distribution of undesirable publications and media, establishes the Board of Censors with powers to classify and ban content, and imposes forfeiture and disposal orders on offending materials, affecting content platforms, streaming services, and media distribution businesses operating in or importing content into Zimbabwe.
