# National Council for Higher Education Act

**Status**: Repealed
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[National Council for Higher Education (NCHE)]]"
  - "[[Ministry of Higher and Tertiary Education, Innovation, Science and Technology Development]]"


## Description
This law was the predecessor to the Zimbabwe Council for Higher Education Act. It was created in 1990 to evaluate applications for private higher education institutions and advise the Minister on higher education policy. It regulated higher education and policy advisory sectors. Investors should care because it was repealed in 2006 due to limited enforcement powers and replaced by the more autonomous ZIMCHE Act, and is now only relevant for legacy institutional query tracking and historical regulatory context.
