# Special Economic Zones Act

**Status**: Repealed
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Zimbabwe Special Economic Zones Authority (ZSEZA)]]"
  - "[[Zimbabwe Investment and Development Agency (ZIDA)]]"


## Description
This law was enacted in November 2016 to establish Special Economic Zones (SEZs) for export-oriented industrial activities. It was created to attract foreign investment, boost exports, and stimulate industrial development through tax incentives and infrastructure support. It regulated export-oriented manufacturing, industrial development, and foreign investment sectors. Investors should care because it created the Zimbabwe Special Economic Zones Authority and Board to administer SEZs, including granting investment licenses and infrastructure permits, but was repealed in February 2020 by the ZIDA Act, which now governs SEZ incentives.
