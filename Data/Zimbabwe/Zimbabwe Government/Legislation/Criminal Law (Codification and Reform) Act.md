# Criminal Law (Codification and Reform) Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[National Prosecuting Authority]]"
  - "[[Zimbabwe Republic Police (ZRP)]]"
  - "[[Judicial Service Commission]]"


## Description
This law codifies the general principles of criminal law and defines offences including fraud, theft, bribery, corruption, and economic crimes. It was created to modernize and consolidate criminal law into a single statute, replacing the fragmented Roman-Dutch common law system. It regulates all sectors through criminal liability for business crimes. Investors should care because it serves as the foundational criminal statute under which business crimes are prosecuted, and intersects with anti-corruption, money laundering, and exchange control enforcement, providing the penal framework for corporate misconduct.
