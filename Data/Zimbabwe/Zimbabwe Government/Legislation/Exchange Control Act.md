# Exchange Control Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe (RBZ)]]"
  - "[[Financial Intelligence Unit]]"


## Description
This law is the principal legislation governing foreign exchange transactions, cross-border payments, and capital flows. It was created to maintain monetary stability, prevent capital flight, and regulate the movement of foreign currency across borders. It regulates banking, finance, foreign investment, mining, manufacturing, and international trade sectors. Investors should care because all foreign investors must obtain exchange control approval for dividend repatriation, loan repayments, royalties, and intercompany transfers, and it imposes strict penalties for externalization and unauthorized foreign currency dealings.
