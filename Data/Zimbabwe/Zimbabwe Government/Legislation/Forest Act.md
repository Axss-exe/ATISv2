# Forest Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Forestry Commission of Zimbabwe]]"


## Description
This law provides for the conservation, management, and sustainable utilization of forest resources. It was created to prevent deforestation, regulate timber extraction, and ensure sustainable use of forest ecosystems. It regulates forestry, agriculture, construction, and biomass energy sectors. Investors should care because it regulates timber harvesting, forest reserve declarations, and licensing of sawmill operations, with direct implications for agriculture, construction materials, and biomass energy operations.
