# Securities Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Securities and Exchange Commission of Zimbabwe (SECZ)]]"


## Description
This law regulates the securities market, including stock exchanges, securities dealers, and collective investment schemes. It was created to ensure fair and transparent capital markets, protect investors, and facilitate corporate fundraising. It regulates capital markets, securities, investment, and corporate finance sectors. Investors should care because it provides for the registration of public offerings, prohibits insider trading, and establishes market conduct rules, underpinning corporate fundraising through capital markets and investor protection mechanisms.
