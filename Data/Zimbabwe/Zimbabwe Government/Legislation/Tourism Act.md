# Tourism Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Zimbabwe Tourism Authority (ZTA)]]"
  - "[[Ministry of Environment, Climate and Wildlife]]"


## Description
This law establishes the Zimbabwe Tourism Authority (ZTA) to regulate and promote the tourism industry. It was created to develop tourism as a major economic driver, ensure quality standards, and market Zimbabwe as a tourist destination. It regulates tourism, hospitality, travel, and eco-tourism sectors. Investors should care because it provides for the licensing of tourism operators, hotels, tour guides, and travel agencies, and governs tourism levies, quality standards, and the marketing of Zimbabwe as a tourist destination.
