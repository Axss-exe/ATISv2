# Education Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education]]"


## Description
This law regulates standard frameworks for schools, training institutions, and curriculum compliance. It was created to ensure quality education, standardize the national curriculum, and govern the administration of public and private schools. It regulates education and interfaces with corporate mining or agriculture houses establishing localized corporate social responsibility (CSR) school infrastructures. Investors should care because it mandates compliance when establishing corporate social responsibility school infrastructures, boarding facilities, or any educational institution.
