# Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe (RBZ)]]"


## Description
This law dictates the monetary policy framework, banking regulations, and exchange controls. It was created to maintain monetary stability, regulate financial institutions, and manage foreign exchange in the economy. It regulates banking, finance, foreign investment, mining, and manufacturing sectors. Investors should care because it governs forex retention ratios, dividend repatriation procedures, and cross-border capital registrations via Exchange Control directives, directly impacting the ability to move capital and profits across borders.
