# Immigration Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Department of Immigration]]"
  - "[[Ministry of Home Affairs and Cultural Heritage]]"


## Description
This law is the principal legislation governing the entry, stay, and exit of foreign nationals. It was created to regulate immigration, ensure national security, and facilitate legitimate travel, residence, and employment of foreign nationals. It regulates all sectors through visa categories for employees, investors, and business visitors. Investors should care because it provides for employment permits, investor permits, residence permits, and student permits, making it a core compliance framework for any business employing expatriates or hosting foreign investors and specialists.
