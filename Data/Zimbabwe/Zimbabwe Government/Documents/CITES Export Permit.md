## CITES Export Permit

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Zimbabwe Parks and Wildlife Management Authority (ZimParks)]]
**Processing Time**: 14 to 30 working days
**Document Type**: Permit


## Description
The **CITES Export Permit** is issued by the **[[Zimbabwe Parks and Wildlife Management Authority (ZimParks)]]** under the **Parks and Wild Life Act [Chapter 20:14]** and **CITES Convention**, authorising the export of wildlife specimens including live animals, animal products, trophies, and plants that are listed under CITES appendices. The permit verifies that the export is legal, non-detrimental to species survival, and compliant with destination country import requirements. For Appendix I species (such as elephants, rhinos, and certain crocodiles), additional scientific and management authority approvals are required, and specimens must be properly marked or tagged for traceability.
