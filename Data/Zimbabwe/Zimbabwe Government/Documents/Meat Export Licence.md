## Meat Export Licence

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Department of Veterinary Services (DVS)]]; [[Local Authority]]
**Processing Time**: 45 to 60 working days
**Document Type**: License


## Description
The **Meat Export Licence** is issued by the **[[Department of Veterinary Services (DVS)]]** with **[[Local Authority]]** endorsement under the **Animal Health Act [Chapter 19:01]** and **Public Health Act [Chapter 15:09]**, authorising abattoirs and meat processing facilities to export beef, pork, poultry, and game meat to international markets. The licence requires compliance with World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH) standards, Hazard Analysis Critical Control Point (HACCP) certification, and destination country import requirements. Facilities must maintain disease-free herds, implement traceability systems from farm to fork, and pass rigorous veterinary inspections of slaughtering, processing, and cold storage operations.
