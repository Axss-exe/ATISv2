## Fishing Permit (Recreational)

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Zimbabwe Parks and Wildlife Management Authority (ZimParks)]]
**Processing Time**: Same day (at point of purchase)
**Document Type**: Permit


## Description
The **Fishing Permit (Recreational)** is issued by the **[[Zimbabwe Parks and Wildlife Management Authority (ZimParks)]]** under the **Parks and Wild Life Act [Chapter 20:14]**, authorising individuals to engage in rod-and-line fishing in public waters including Lake Kariba, the Zambezi River, and national park dams. The permit specifies the number of rods, hook limits, bag limits, and permitted fishing areas, with separate categories for ordinary daily permits and special permits for trout fisheries in the Eastern Highlands. Conservation fees apply in addition to permit costs, and anglers must adhere to catch-and-release protocols for protected species and closed seasons for breeding protection.
