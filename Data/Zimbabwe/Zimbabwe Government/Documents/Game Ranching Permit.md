## Game Ranching Permit

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Zimbabwe Parks and Wildlife Management Authority (ZimParks)]]
**Processing Time**: 30 to 60 working days
**Document Type**: Permit


## Description
The **Game Ranching Permit** is issued by the **[[Zimbabwe Parks and Wildlife Management Authority (ZimParks)]]** under the **Parks and Wild Life Act [Chapter 20:14]**, authorising private landowners to breed, rear, and sustainably utilise wildlife species on their properties for commercial purposes including trophy hunting, meat production, live sales, and tourism. The permit requires proof of adequate fencing, water provision, habitat suitability, and veterinary care capacity. Holders must maintain species-specific stocking rates, implement wildlife management plans, and comply with quota systems for offtake. The permit facilitates the integration of wildlife conservation with rural land use, contributing to biodiversity protection and community livelihoods.
