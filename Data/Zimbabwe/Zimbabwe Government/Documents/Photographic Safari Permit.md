## Photographic Safari Permit

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Zimbabwe Parks and Wildlife Management Authority (ZimParks)]]
**Processing Time**: 14 to 21 working days
**Document Type**: Permit


## Description
The **Photographic Safari Permit** is issued by the **[[Zimbabwe Parks and Wildlife Management Authority (ZimParks)]]** under the **Parks and Wild Life Act [Chapter 20:14]**, authorising tour operators and individuals to conduct non-consumptive wildlife photography tours and filming activities in national parks, safari areas, and wildlife conservancies. The permit distinguishes photographic tourism from hunting activities and mandates compliance with wildlife disturbance minimisation protocols, drone usage restrictions (requiring separate CAAZ approval), and environmental protection standards. Permit holders must adhere to designated viewing distances, off-road driving restrictions, and waste management requirements while operating in protected areas.
