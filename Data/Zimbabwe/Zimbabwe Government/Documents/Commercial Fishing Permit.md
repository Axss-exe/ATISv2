## Commercial Fishing Permit

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Zimbabwe Parks and Wildlife Management Authority (ZimParks)]]
**Processing Time**: 21 to 30 working days
**Document Type**: Permit


## Description
The **Commercial Fishing Permit** is issued by the **[[Zimbabwe Parks and Wildlife Management Authority (ZimParks)]]** under the **Parks and Wild Life Act [Chapter 20:14]**, authorising professional fishers to catch and sell fish using nets, traps, or other approved methods in designated waters including Lake Kariba, Lake Chivero, and state dams. The permit categories include gillnet permits, kapenta (small pelagic fish) fishing permits, and crayfish permits, each with specific gear specifications, catch quotas, and seasonal restrictions. Commercial fishers must register their vessels, maintain catch logs, and comply with closed seasons designed to protect breeding stocks and ensure sustainable fisheries management.
