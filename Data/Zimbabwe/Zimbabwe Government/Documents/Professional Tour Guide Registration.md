## Professional Tour Guide Registration

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Zimbabwe Tourism Authority (ZTA)]]
**Processing Time**: 14 to 21 working days
**Document Type**: Registration


## Description
The **Professional Tour Guide Registration** is a personal credential issued by the **[[Zimbabwe Tourism Authority (ZTA)]]** under the **Tourism Act [Chapter 14:20]**, certifying individuals to lead and interpret tours at Zimbabwe's tourist destinations. Registration requires demonstration of knowledge in Zimbabwean history, culture, wildlife, and ecology, along with first aid certification and communication skills. Guides must adhere to a code of conduct, maintain continuous professional development, and wear identification badges while conducting tours. The registration distinguishes professional guides from informal operators and ensures visitor safety and quality experiences.
