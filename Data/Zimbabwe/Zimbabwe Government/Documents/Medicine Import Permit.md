## Medicine Import Permit

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Medicines Control Authority of Zimbabwe (MCAZ)]]
**Processing Time**: 5 to 10 working days
**Document Type**: Permit


## Description
The **Medicine Import Permit** is a regulatory authorisation issued by the **[[Medicines Control Authority of Zimbabwe (MCAZ)]]** under the **Medicines and Allied Substances Control Act [Chapter 15:03]**, permitting the lawful importation of registered pharmaceutical products into Zimbabwe. The permit ensures that imported medicines meet national quality, safety, and efficacy standards, and that only authorised importers—such as licensed wholesale dealers, pharmacies, manufacturers, or approved medical practitioners—bring medicines into the country. Each consignment requires a separate permit specifying the product name, quantity, manufacturer, supplier, and port of entry, with a verification fee of 0.05% of CIF value payable before clearance.
