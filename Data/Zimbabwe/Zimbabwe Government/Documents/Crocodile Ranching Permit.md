## Crocodile Ranching Permit

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Zimbabwe Parks and Wildlife Management Authority (ZimParks)]]
**Processing Time**: 30 to 60 working days
**Document Type**: Permit


## Description
The **Crocodile Ranching Permit** is issued by the **[[Zimbabwe Parks and Wildlife Management Authority (ZimParks)]]** under the **Parks and Wild Life Act [Chapter 20:14]** and **CITES Convention**, authorising the breeding, rearing, and commercial utilisation of Nile crocodiles (*Crocodylus niloticus*) in controlled ranching environments. Zimbabwe's crocodile population was transferred to CITES Appendix II in 1983, enabling regulated international trade in skins and meat from ranched specimens. The permit requires proof of secure breeding facilities, veterinary care protocols, egg collection permits from wild nests, skin tagging systems for traceability, and compliance with animal welfare standards. Ranchers must maintain breeding records, mortality logs, and CITES transaction documentation for all commercial sales.
