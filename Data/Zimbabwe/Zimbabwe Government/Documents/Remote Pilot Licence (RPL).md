## Remote Pilot Licence (RPL)

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Civil Aviation Authority of Zimbabwe (CAAZ)]]
**Processing Time**: 14 to 21 working days
**Document Type**: License


## Description
The **Remote Pilot Licence (RPL)** is issued by the **[[Civil Aviation Authority of Zimbabwe (CAAZ)]]** under the **Civil Aviation (Remotely Piloted Aircraft Systems) Regulations**, authorising individuals to operate drones and unmanned aerial vehicles for commercial, recreational, or research purposes. The licence requires completion of ground school training, practical flight instruction, a skills test by a CAAZ-designated examiner, and a Class 4 medical certificate. Holders must comply with airspace restrictions, altitude limitations, privacy regulations, and no-fly zones around airports, military installations, and sensitive government facilities.
