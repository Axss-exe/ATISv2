## Clinical Trial Authorisation

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Medicines Control Authority of Zimbabwe (MCAZ)]]
**Processing Time**: 60 to 90 working days
**Document Type**: Approval


## Description
The **Clinical Trial Authorisation** is a regulatory approval granted by the **[[Medicines Control Authority of Zimbabwe (MCAZ)]]** under the **Medicines and Allied Substances Control Act [Chapter 15:03]**, permitting the conduct of clinical research involving human subjects to evaluate the safety, efficacy, or quality of medicines, vaccines, or medical devices. The authorisation requires submission of a comprehensive trial protocol, ethics committee approval, investigator qualifications, informed consent procedures, and data safety monitoring plans. MCAZ reviews applications to ensure participant protection, scientific validity, and compliance with international good clinical practice standards before granting permission to commence trials.
