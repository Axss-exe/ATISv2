## Building Plan Approval

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Local Authority]]
**Processing Time**: 30 to 45 working days
**Document Type**: Approval


## Description
The **Building Plan Approval** is a planning and construction authorisation issued by the **[[Local Authority]]** under the **Regional Town and Country Planning Act [Chapter 29:12]** and **Urban Councils Act [Chapter 29:15]**, certifying that proposed building designs comply with zoning regulations, structural safety standards, and urban planning requirements. The approval process involves review of architectural drawings, structural engineering calculations, stormwater drainage plans, and fire safety provisions by council technical departments including Town Planning, Works, and Fire Services. While Zimbabwe abolished building permit requirements at the local authority level in May 2026 for property development projects, building plan approval remains a prerequisite for construction commencement to ensure compliance with national building by-laws and safety standards.
