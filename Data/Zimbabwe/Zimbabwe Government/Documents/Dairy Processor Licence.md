## Dairy Processor Licence

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Dairy Services]]; [[Local Authority]]
**Processing Time**: 30 to 45 working days
**Document Type**: License


## Description
The **Dairy Processor Licence** is issued by **[[Dairy Services]]** with **[[Local Authority]]** health clearance under the **Dairy Act [Chapter 18:06]** and **Public Health Act [Chapter 15:09]**, authorising entities to process, package, and distribute milk and dairy products including cheese, yoghurt, butter, and ice cream. The licence mandates compliance with pasteurisation standards, cold chain maintenance, hygiene protocols, and product labelling requirements. Processors must maintain traceability records from farm to consumer, undergo regular microbiological testing of products, and ensure that all milk sourced from registered dairy farmers meets somatic cell count and bacterial quality standards.
