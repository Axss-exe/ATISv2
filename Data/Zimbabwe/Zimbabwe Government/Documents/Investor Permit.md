## Investor Permit

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Zimbabwe Investment and Development Agency (ZIDA)]]; [[Department of Immigration]]
**Processing Time**: 30 to 60 working days
**Document Type**: Permit


## Description
The **Investor Permit** is a combined authorisation processed through the **[[Zimbabwe Investment and Development Agency (ZIDA)]]** and endorsed by the **[[Department of Immigration]]** under the **Zimbabwe Investment and Development Agency Act [Chapter 14:37]** and **Immigration Act [Chapter 4:02]**, granting foreign investors the right to reside and oversee business ventures in Zimbabwe. The permit is available to individuals who have made significant capital investments, typically exceeding USD 100,000, and who hold a valid ZIDA Investment Licence. It facilitates investor residence, work authorisation, and preferential treatment including duty rebates on capital equipment, tax incentives, and streamlined regulatory procedures.
