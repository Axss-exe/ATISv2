## ZIMCHE Programme Accreditation

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Zimbabwe Council for Higher Education (ZIMCHE)]]
**Processing Time**: 6 to 12 months per programme
**Document Type**: Approval


## Description
The **ZIMCHE Programme Accreditation** is granted by the **[[Zimbabwe Council for Higher Education (ZIMCHE)]]** for individual academic programmes offered by higher education institutions. It ensures that each programme meets national standards for curriculum content, learning outcomes, staff qualifications, teaching and learning resources, assessment methods, and graduate competencies. All degree and diploma programmes must be accredited before enrolment of students, and institutions must seek re-accreditation for programme modifications.
