## Safari Operator Licence

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Zimbabwe Parks and Wildlife Management Authority (ZimParks)]]
**Processing Time**: 30 to 60 working days
**Document Type**: License


## Description
The **Safari Operator Licence** is issued by the **[[Zimbabwe Parks and Wildlife Management Authority (ZimParks)]]** under the **Parks and Wild Life Act [Chapter 20:14]**, authorising entities to organise and conduct hunting safaris, photographic safaris, and wildlife viewing expeditions in designated safari areas, communal lands, and private conservancies. The licence requires proof of professional hunter employment, adequate camping and hunting equipment, liability insurance coverage, and compliance with conservation quotas and ethical standards. Safari operators must maintain detailed client records, ensure all hunting activities are properly licensed, and contribute to community benefit-sharing arrangements in communal safari areas.
