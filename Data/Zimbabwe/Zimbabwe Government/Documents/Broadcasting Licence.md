## Broadcasting Licence

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Broadcasting Authority of Zimbabwe (BAZ)]]
**Processing Time**: 60 to 120 working days
**Document Type**: License


## Description
The **Broadcasting Licence** is a regulatory authorisation granted by the **[[Broadcasting Authority of Zimbabwe (BAZ)]]** under the **Broadcasting Services Act [Chapter 12:06]**, permitting entities to provide broadcasting services including commercial radio, community radio, subscription television, and digital broadcasting. The licence ensures compliance with content standards, frequency allocation, technical specifications, and public interest obligations. It mandates adherence to national broadcasting codes, local content quotas, and emergency communication protocols, while providing a framework for fair competition and media diversity in Zimbabwe's broadcasting landscape.
