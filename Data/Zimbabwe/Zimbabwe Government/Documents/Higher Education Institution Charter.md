## Higher Education Institution Charter

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Zimbabwe Council for Higher Education (ZIMCHE)]]; [[President of Zimbabwe]]
**Processing Time**: 12 to 24 months
**Document Type**: Authorization


## Description
The **Higher Education Institution Charter** is the foundational legal instrument granted by the **[[President of Zimbabwe]]** on the advice of the **[[Zimbabwe Council for Higher Education (ZIMCHE)]]** under the **Zimbabwe Council for Higher Education Act [Chapter 25:27]**. It establishes a university or institution of higher learning as a legal entity, defining its mandate, governance structure, academic autonomy, and degree-awarding powers. The charter is the highest level of authorisation for higher education institutions and is prerequisite for offering degree programmes. Private universities must first obtain provisional registration from ZIMCHE before applying for a charter.
