## Liquor Licence

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Liquor Licensing Board]]; [[Local Authority]]
**Processing Time**: 60 to 90 working days
**Document Type**: License


## Description
The **Liquor Licence** is a statutory authorisation issued by the **[[Liquor Licensing Board]]** in conjunction with **[[Local Authority]]** approval under the **Liquor Act [Chapter 14:12]**, permitting the sale of alcoholic beverages through various categories including hotel, restaurant, bottle store, bar, and wholesale licences. The licence mandates compliance with zoning restrictions, public interest considerations, health and safety standards, and trading hour limitations. Applicants must publish intent in the Government Gazette and local newspaper, obtain police clearance, submit approved building plans, and demonstrate that the licence will not negatively affect nearby residents, schools, or religious institutions.
