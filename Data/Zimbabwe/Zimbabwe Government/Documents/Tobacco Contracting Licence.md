## Tobacco Contracting Licence

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Tobacco Industry and Marketing Board (TIMB)]]
**Processing Time**: 21 to 30 working days
**Document Type**: License


## Description
The **Tobacco Contracting Licence** is granted by the **[[Tobacco Industry and Marketing Board (TIMB)]]** under the **Tobacco Marketing and Levy Act**, authorising tobacco merchants and companies to enter into forward contracts with registered growers for the supply of inputs (seed, fertiliser, chemicals, and curing fuel) in exchange for exclusive rights to purchase the produced leaf. The licence mandates compliance with the Contractors Compliance Administration Framework, including minimum input package requirements, timely input delivery, and fair pricing mechanisms. TIMB monitors contractor performance to prevent side-marketing, input default, and exploitative contract terms that disadvantage smallholder farmers.
