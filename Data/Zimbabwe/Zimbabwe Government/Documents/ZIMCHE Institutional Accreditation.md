## ZIMCHE Institutional Accreditation

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Zimbabwe Council for Higher Education (ZIMCHE)]]
**Processing Time**: 12 to 18 months
**Document Type**: Certificate


## Description
The **ZIMCHE Institutional Accreditation** is a quality assurance certification granted by the **[[Zimbabwe Council for Higher Education (ZIMCHE)]]** under the **Zimbabwe Council for Higher Education Act [Chapter 25:27]**. It evaluates and accredits both public and private higher education institutions against standards for institutional quality assurance, programme relevance, financial resources, staff calibre, physical infrastructure, equipment, strategic leadership, programme length and depth, examination standards, management efficiency, and peer evaluation. Accreditation is mandatory for all higher education institutions and their programmes.
