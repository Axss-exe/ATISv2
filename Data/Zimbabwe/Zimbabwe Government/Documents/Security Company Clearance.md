## Security Company Clearance

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Zimbabwe Republic Police (ZRP)]]
**Processing Time**: 30 to 60 working days
**Document Type**: Clearance


## Description
The **Security Company Clearance** is a mandatory authorisation issued by the **[[Zimbabwe Republic Police (ZRP)]]** under the **Private Investigators and Security Guards Act [Chapter 27:10]**, permitting entities to provide security services including manned guarding, alarm response, and asset protection. The clearance involves comprehensive vetting of the company, its directors, and proposed security personnel to ensure integrity, competency, and absence of criminal records. Holders must maintain guard vetting records, adhere to National Employment Council wage standards, provide distinct uniforms distinguishable from police or military attire, and comply with firearms licensing requirements for armed response services.
