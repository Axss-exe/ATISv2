## Hotel Operators Licence

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Local Authority]]; [[Zimbabwe Tourism Authority (ZTA)]]
**Processing Time**: 30 to 60 working days
**Document Type**: License


## Description
The **Hotel Operators Licence** is a dual-requirement permit issued by the **[[Local Authority]]** under the **Urban Councils Act [Chapter 29:15]** and registered with the **[[Zimbabwe Tourism Authority (ZTA)]]** under the **Tourism Act [Chapter 14:20]**, authorising the operation of hotels, lodges, and guest houses. The licence mandates compliance with building safety standards, fire prevention measures, food hygiene, room quality grading, and staff training requirements. ZTA conducts star-grading assessments based on amenities, service levels, and facilities, while local authorities enforce zoning, health, and structural compliance. Annual renewal requires proof of continued adherence to both local authority and tourism sector standards.
