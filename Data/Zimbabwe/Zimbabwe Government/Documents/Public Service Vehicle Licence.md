## Public Service Vehicle Licence

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Vehicle Inspection Department (VID)]]; [[Local Authority]]
**Processing Time**: 14 to 30 working days
**Document Type**: License


## Description
The **Public Service Vehicle Licence** is issued by the **[[Vehicle Inspection Department (VID)]]** with **[[Local Authority]]** endorsement under the **Road Traffic Act [Chapter 13:11]** and **Urban Councils Act [Chapter 29:15]**, authorising vehicles to transport passengers for reward including buses, taxis, kombis, and commuter omnibuses. The licence requires comprehensive vehicle inspection for passenger safety features, driver competency certification, route approval from local authorities, and compliance with passenger capacity limits. Holders must display route permits, maintain vehicle condition standards, adhere to designated stopping points, and comply with fare regulation frameworks established by transport authorities.
