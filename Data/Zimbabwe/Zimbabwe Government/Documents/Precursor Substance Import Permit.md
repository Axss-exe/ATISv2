## Description
The **Precursor Substance Import Permit** is issued by the **[[Medicines Control Authority of Zimbabwe (MCAZ)]]** under the **Medicines and Allied Substances Control Act [Chapter 15:03]** and **Dangerous Drugs Act [Chapter 15:02]**, authorising the importation of chemical substances that can be used in the manufacture of illicit drugs, including hydrochloric acid, sulphuric acid, and other controlled precursors. The permit implements Zimbabwe's international obligations under the United Nations Convention Against Illicit Traffic in Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances, requiring end-user verification, storage security documentation, and transaction record-keeping. Importers must demonstrate legitimate industrial or pharmaceutical use, maintain secure storage facilities, and report all transactions to MCAZ and law enforcement agencies.
