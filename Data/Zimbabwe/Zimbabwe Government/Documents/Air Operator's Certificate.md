## Air Operator's Certificate

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Civil Aviation Authority of Zimbabwe (CAAZ)]]
**Processing Time**: 90 working days
**Document Type**: Certificate


## Description
The **Air Operator's Certificate** is a mandatory certification issued by the **[[Civil Aviation Authority of Zimbabwe (CAAZ)]]** under the **Civil Aviation Act [Chapter 13:16]**, authorising an entity to operate commercial air services within Zimbabwean airspace. The certificate certifies that the applicant possesses adequate financial resources, properly maintained aircraft, qualified flight crew, and compliant operational procedures to ensure passenger and cargo safety. It specifies the types of aircraft, routes, and operational conditions permitted, and requires ongoing compliance with CAAZ safety audits, maintenance schedules, and crew training standards. The certificate is essential for airlines, charter operators, and cargo carriers seeking to conduct remunerated aviation activities.
