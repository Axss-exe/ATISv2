## Hawker's Licence

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Local Authority]]
**Processing Time**: 3 to 7 working days
**Document Type**: License


## Description
The **Hawker's Licence** is issued by the **[[Local Authority]]** under the **Shop Licences Act [Chapter 14:17]** and **Urban Councils Act [Chapter 29:15]**, authorising mobile vendors and street traders to conduct business in designated vending zones without fixed premises. The licence is more accessible and affordable than fixed-premises licences but restricts trading to council-approved locations, times, and product categories. Holders must comply with hygiene standards for food vendors, waste disposal requirements, and public order regulations, while avoiding obstruction of pedestrian and vehicular traffic.
