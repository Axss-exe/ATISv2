## University Charter

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[President of Zimbabwe]] (on advice of [[Zimbabwe Council for Higher Education (ZIMCHE)]])
**Processing Time**: 24 to 48 months
**Document Type**: Authorization


## Description
The **University Charter** is the supreme legal instrument establishing a university in Zimbabwe, granted by the **[[President of Zimbabwe]]** on the recommendation of the **[[Zimbabwe Council for Higher Education (ZIMCHE)]]** and the responsible Minister. It defines the university's name, objects, powers, governance structure (Council, Senate, Vice-Chancellor), academic freedom, land and property rights, and relationship with government. Public universities receive their charters through Acts of Parliament, while private universities must first obtain ZIMCHE provisional registration and then a Presidential Charter.
