## Medicine Export Permit

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Medicines Control Authority of Zimbabwe (MCAZ)]]
**Processing Time**: 5 to 10 working days
**Document Type**: Permit


## Description
The **Medicine Export Permit** is issued by the **[[Medicines Control Authority of Zimbabwe (MCAZ)]]** pursuant to the **Medicines and Allied Substances Control Act [Chapter 15:03]**, authorising the export of registered pharmaceutical products from Zimbabwe. The permit verifies that exported medicines comply with destination country requirements, maintains traceability of pharmaceutical supply chains, and prevents the diversion of essential medicines from domestic markets. Exporters must be licensed wholesale dealers or manufacturers appointed by the product principal, and each shipment requires detailed documentation including product specifications, quantities, destination, and end-user verification.
