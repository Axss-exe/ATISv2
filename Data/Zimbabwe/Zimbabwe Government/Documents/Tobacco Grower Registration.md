## Tobacco Grower Registration

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Tobacco Industry and Marketing Board (TIMB)]]
**Processing Time**: 14 to 21 working days
**Document Type**: Registration


## Description
The **Tobacco Grower Registration** is a compulsory credential issued by the **[[Tobacco Industry and Marketing Board (TIMB)]]** under the **Tobacco Marketing and Levy Act**, authorising farmers to cultivate, cure, and market tobacco in Zimbabwe. Registration categorises growers by scale—smallholder, medium, or large—and links them to either auction floor sales or contract farming arrangements with tobacco merchants. The registration ensures traceability of tobacco production, compliance with sustainable curing practices (including anti-deforestation measures), and adherence to child labour prohibitions. TIMB maintains a centralised database of all registered growers to regulate marketing channels and levy collection.
