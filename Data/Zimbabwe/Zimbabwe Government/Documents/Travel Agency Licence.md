## Travel Agency Licence

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Zimbabwe Tourism Authority (ZTA)]]
**Processing Time**: 21 to 30 working days
**Document Type**: License


## Description
The **Travel Agency Licence** is issued by the **[[Zimbabwe Tourism Authority (ZTA)]]** under the **Tourism Act [Chapter 14:20]**, authorising entities to act as intermediaries in the sale of travel products including airline tickets, accommodation bookings, tour packages, and travel insurance. The licence requires demonstration of financial solvency, bonding arrangements to protect client funds, staff competency in travel reservations systems, and compliance with International Air Transport Association (IATA) regulations where applicable. Agencies must maintain transparent pricing, issue proper documentation to travellers, and handle complaints in accordance with consumer protection standards.
