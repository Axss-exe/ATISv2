# Lake Kariba (Dam Wall)

**Infrastructure Type:**  Dam
**Operational Status:** `operational`
**Operated By**: [[Zambezi River Authority (ZRA)]]
**Value Is**: One of the world's largest man-made reservoirs, created by a double-curvature concrete arch dam. Its existence provides the water head for Kariba South hydro, fisheries for 300,000+ people, and a transport corridor across the Zambezi. Without Kariba, Zimbabwe loses 40–50% of its grid capacity instantly, and the Zambezi Valley's mining and tourism economies collapse. The dam's structural integrity is monitored continuously; a failure would be a continental-scale catastrophe affecting Mozambique, Malawi, and Zambia downstream. Siltation and climate-induced water level volatility are long-term existential risks.


## Summary

One of the world's largest man-made reservoirs, created by a double-curvature concrete arch dam. Its existence provides the water head for Kariba South hydro, fisheries for 300,000+ people, and a transport corridor across the Zambezi. Without Kariba, Zimbabwe loses 40–50% of its grid capacity instantly, and the Zambezi Valley's mining and tourism economies collapse. The dam's structural integrity is monitored continuously; a failure would be a continental-scale catastrophe affecting Mozambique, Malawi, and Zambia downstream. Siltation and climate-induced water level volatility are long-term existential risks.

#### Technical Notes: 
185 km³ storage; 1,050 MW hydro capacity
