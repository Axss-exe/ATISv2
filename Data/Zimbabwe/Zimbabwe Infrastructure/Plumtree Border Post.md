# Plumtree Border Post

**Infrastructure Type:**  Border Crossing
**Operational Status:** `operational`
**Operated By**: [[Zimbabwe Revenue Authority (ZRA)]]
**Value Is**: The western border with Botswana on the Trans-Kalahari Highway to Walvis Bay (Namibia). Its existence provides an alternative export route for platinum, nickel, and chrome to Namibian ports, reducing South African port dependence. Without it, western Zimbabwe's mining belt faces a single-port bottleneck and potential sanctions or congestion risk at Durban. Traffic volume is low relative to Beitbridge, reflecting the longer distance to Walvis Bay and limited Namibian port capacity for bulk minerals.


## Summary

The western border with Botswana on the Trans-Kalahari Highway to Walvis Bay (Namibia). Its existence provides an alternative export route for platinum, nickel, and chrome to Namibian ports, reducing South African port dependence. Without it, western Zimbabwe's mining belt faces a single-port bottleneck and potential sanctions or congestion risk at Durban. Traffic volume is low relative to Beitbridge, reflecting the longer distance to Walvis Bay and limited Namibian port capacity for bulk minerals.
