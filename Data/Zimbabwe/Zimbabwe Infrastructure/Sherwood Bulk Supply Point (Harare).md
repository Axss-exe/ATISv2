# Sherwood Bulk Supply Point (Harare)

**Infrastructure Type:**  Electrical Substation
**Operational Status:** `operational`
**Operated By**: [[Zimbabwe Electricity Transmission and Distribution Company (ZETDC)]]
**Value Is**: The primary grid node for Harare and northern Zimbabwe. Its existence enables step-down transformation for Mashonaland West's emerging lithium province (Arcadia, Bikita, Zulu). Without Sherwood, there is no grid entry point for 132 kV distribution to these mining projects. The substation's capacity constraints are already forcing lithium developers to consider private transmission lines or on-site solar — adding \$20–50M to project capex. Copper theft and transformer overload are chronic operational risks.


## Summary

The primary grid node for Harare and northern Zimbabwe. Its existence enables step-down transformation for Mashonaland West's emerging lithium province (Arcadia, Bikita, Zulu). Without Sherwood, there is no grid entry point for 132 kV distribution to these mining projects. The substation's capacity constraints are already forcing lithium developers to consider private transmission lines or on-site solar — adding \$20–50M to project capex. Copper theft and transformer overload are chronic operational risks.

#### Technical Notes: 
Multiple 330/132/33 kV transformers
