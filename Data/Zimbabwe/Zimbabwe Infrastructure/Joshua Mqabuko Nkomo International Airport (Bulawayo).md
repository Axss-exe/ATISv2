# Joshua Mqabuko Nkomo International Airport (Bulawayo)

**Infrastructure Type:**  Airport
**Operational Status:** `operational`
**Operated By**: [[Airports Company of Zimbabwe (ACZ)]]
**Value Is**: Zimbabwe's second city airport serving the southern mining belt. Its existence provides direct access to the Unki platinum mine, chrome smelters, and the Beitbridge border logistics hub without transiting Harare. Without it, southern mining executives face 6-hour road transfers, and emergency spare parts deliveries are delayed by a full day. Limited international routes (primarily Johannesburg) restrict its utility for non-regional investors.


## Summary

Zimbabwe's second city airport serving the southern mining belt. Its existence provides direct access to the Unki platinum mine, chrome smelters, and the Beitbridge border logistics hub without transiting Harare. Without it, southern mining executives face 6-hour road transfers, and emergency spare parts deliveries are delayed by a full day. Limited international routes (primarily Johannesburg) restrict its utility for non-regional investors.

#### Technical Notes: 
2,580 m runway; domestic and limited international
