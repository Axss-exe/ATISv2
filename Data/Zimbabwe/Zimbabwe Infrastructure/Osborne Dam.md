# Osborne Dam

**Infrastructure Type:**  Dam
**Operational Status:** `operational`
**Operated By**: [[Zimbabwe National Water Authority (ZINWA)]]
**Value Is**: A multi-purpose dam on the Odzi River supplying water to Mutare and surrounding agricultural areas. Its existence ensures water security for Zimbabwe's third-largest city and the eastern highlands' mining services hub. Without it, Mutare's industrial base (including mining equipment suppliers and timber processing) would face severe water rationing. The dam also supports small-scale irrigation and fisheries, providing food security for mining communities.


## Summary

A multi-purpose dam on the Odzi River supplying water to Mutare and surrounding agricultural areas. Its existence ensures water security for Zimbabwe's third-largest city and the eastern highlands' mining services hub. Without it, Mutare's industrial base (including mining equipment suppliers and timber processing) would face severe water rationing. The dam also supports small-scale irrigation and fisheries, providing food security for mining communities.
