# A5 / R2 Highway (Harare–Bulawayo–Plumtree)

**Infrastructure Type:**  Highway
**Operational Status:** `partially_operational`
**Operated By**: [[Zimbabwe National Road Administration (ZINARA)]]
**Value Is**: The principal highway to Botswana and Namibia via the Trans-Kalahari Corridor. Its existence provides an alternative export route for platinum, nickel, and chrome to Walvis Bay (Namibia), reducing dependence on South African ports. Without it, western Zimbabwe's mining belt faces a single-port bottleneck. The road's condition has deteriorated to the point where heavy haul trucks limit speed to 40 km/h, increasing transport costs by 30–50% and extending delivery times.


## Summary

The principal highway to Botswana and Namibia via the Trans-Kalahari Corridor. Its existence provides an alternative export route for platinum, nickel, and chrome to Walvis Bay (Namibia), reducing dependence on South African ports. Without it, western Zimbabwe's mining belt faces a single-port bottleneck. The road's condition has deteriorated to the point where heavy haul trucks limit speed to 40 km/h, increasing transport costs by 30–50% and extending delivery times.
