# Maputo Corridor (Beira/Machipanda Line)

**Infrastructure Type:**  Transport Corridor
**Operational Status:** `partially_operational`
**Operated By**: NRZ / CFM (Mozambique)
**Value Is**: The strategic eastward line to the Indian Ocean port of Beira via the Machipanda border. Its existence provides Zimbabwe's shortest maritime access (600 km to Beira vs. 1,000+ km to Durban), critical for bulk chrome and nickel export competitiveness. Without it, Zimbabwe is entirely dependent on South African ports, creating geopolitical and logistics risk. The line's condition deteriorated after Cyclone Idai (2019), and the Mozambique section suffers from chronic underinvestment. Gauge differences and border delays at Machipanda further reduce competitiveness.


## Summary

The strategic eastward line to the Indian Ocean port of Beira via the Machipanda border. Its existence provides Zimbabwe's shortest maritime access (600 km to Beira vs. 1,000+ km to Durban), critical for bulk chrome and nickel export competitiveness. Without it, Zimbabwe is entirely dependent on South African ports, creating geopolitical and logistics risk. The line's condition deteriorated after Cyclone Idai (2019), and the Mozambique section suffers from chronic underinvestment. Gauge differences and border delays at Machipanda further reduce competitiveness.

#### Technical Notes: 
Connects to Mozambique Ports and Railways
