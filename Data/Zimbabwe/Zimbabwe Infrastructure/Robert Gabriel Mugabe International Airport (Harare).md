# Robert Gabriel Mugabe International Airport (Harare)

**Infrastructure Type:**  Airport
**Operational Status:** `operational`
**Operated By**: [[Airports Company of Zimbabwe (ACZ)]]
**Value Is**: Zimbabwe's principal international gateway and the only airport with regular intercontinental connections. Its existence enables executive travel for mining investors, urgent spare parts import, and high-value mineral sample export. Without it, mining companies would face 6–8 hour drives to Victoria Falls or Lusaka for international flights, crippling deal-making velocity and emergency response times. The 2018 terminal upgrade (Chinese-funded) improved capacity but landing fees remain among the highest in the region, deterring low-cost carriers.


## Summary

Zimbabwe's principal international gateway and the only airport with regular intercontinental connections. Its existence enables executive travel for mining investors, urgent spare parts import, and high-value mineral sample export. Without it, mining companies would face 6–8 hour drives to Victoria Falls or Lusaka for international flights, crippling deal-making velocity and emergency response times. The 2018 terminal upgrade (Chinese-funded) improved capacity but landing fees remain among the highest in the region, deterring low-cost carriers.

#### Technical Notes: 
2,500 m runway; international terminal upgraded 2018
