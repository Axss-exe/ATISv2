# Port of Durban (South Africa — Secondary Zimbabwe Access)

**Infrastructure Type:**  Seaport
**Operational Status:** `operational`
**Operated By**: [[Transnet National Ports Authority]]
**Value Is**: Africa's busiest container port and Zimbabwe's primary alternative for containerized mining equipment, spare parts, and high-value exports. Its existence provides a reliable, high-capacity backup when Beira is congested or cyclone-damaged. Without Durban, Zimbabwe would have no container port access for critical mining inputs, and PGM exports would face severe bottlenecks. The 1,000+ km distance via Beitbridge adds \$1,500–3,000 per container in transport costs. Transnet's operational constraints (locomotive shortages, port congestion) create periodic delays.


## Summary

Africa's busiest container port and Zimbabwe's primary alternative for containerized mining equipment, spare parts, and high-value exports. Its existence provides a reliable, high-capacity backup when Beira is congested or cyclone-damaged. Without Durban, Zimbabwe would have no container port access for critical mining inputs, and PGM exports would face severe bottlenecks. The 1,000+ km distance via Beitbridge adds \$1,500–3,000 per container in transport costs. Transnet's operational constraints (locomotive shortages, port congestion) create periodic delays.

#### Technical Notes: 
80+ million tonnes/year; deepest container terminal in Southern Africa
