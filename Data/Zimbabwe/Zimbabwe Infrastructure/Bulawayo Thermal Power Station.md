# Bulawayo Thermal Power Station

**Infrastructure Type:**  Power Station
**Operational Status:** `dormant / under_rehabilitation`
**Operated By**: [[Zimbabwe Power Company (ZPC)]]
**Value Is**: One of Zimbabwe's four "small thermals" commissioned between 1946–1957. Its existence historically anchored the southern region's industrial and mining power supply. Without functional Bulawayo thermal, the Midlands and Matabeleland mining belts rely on long-distance transmission from Hwange and Kariba, creating voltage instability and transmission losses. Rehabilitation would provide grid redundancy for chrome and nickel operations and reduce reliance on the fragile Central Transmission Corridor. Current dormancy represents a stranded asset and a lost opportunity for regional energy security.


## Summary

One of Zimbabwe's four "small thermals" commissioned between 1946–1957. Its existence historically anchored the southern region's industrial and mining power supply. Without functional Bulawayo thermal, the Midlands and Matabeleland mining belts rely on long-distance transmission from Hwange and Kariba, creating voltage instability and transmission losses. Rehabilitation would provide grid redundancy for chrome and nickel operations and reduce reliance on the fragile Central Transmission Corridor. Current dormancy represents a stranded asset and a lost opportunity for regional energy security.
