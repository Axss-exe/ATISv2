# Beitbridge-Bulawayo Railway (BBR)

**Infrastructure Type:**  Railway Line
**Operational Status:** `operational`
**Operated By**: [Beitbridge-Bulawayo Railway]]
**Value Is**: A privately built direct line from Bulawayo to the South African border at Beitbridge, bypassing the longer Plumtree route. Its existence provides the shortest rail path for Zimbabwe's mineral exports to Richards Bay and Durban, and for South African mining equipment imports. Without BBR, chrome and coal exports would face an additional 200 km of transit time and cost. The 30-year concession (awarded 1999) expires around 2029, creating a major ownership and investment uncertainty that affects long-term mining logistics planning.


## Summary

A privately built direct line from Bulawayo to the South African border at Beitbridge, bypassing the longer Plumtree route. Its existence provides the shortest rail path for Zimbabwe's mineral exports to Richards Bay and Durban, and for South African mining equipment imports. Without BBR, chrome and coal exports would face an additional 200 km of transit time and cost. The 30-year concession (awarded 1999) expires around 2029, creating a major ownership and investment uncertainty that affects long-term mining logistics planning.
