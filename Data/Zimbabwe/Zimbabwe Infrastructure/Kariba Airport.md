# Kariba Airport

**Infrastructure Type:**  Airport
**Operational Status:** `operational`
**Operated By**: [[Airports Company of Zimbabwe (ACZ)]]
**Value Is**: A regional airport serving Lake Kariba and the Zambezi Valley. Its existence provides charter access for geological surveys, tourism, and emergency services in the northern mining frontier. Without it, the Zambezi Valley would be accessible only by 8-hour road from Harare or ferry from Zambia, isolating exploration projects and emergency response. The runway length restricts aircraft size, limiting cargo capacity.


## Summary

A regional airport serving Lake Kariba and the Zambezi Valley. Its existence provides charter access for geological surveys, tourism, and emergency services in the northern mining frontier. Without it, the Zambezi Valley would be accessible only by 8-hour road from Harare or ferry from Zambia, isolating exploration projects and emergency response. The runway length restricts aircraft size, limiting cargo capacity.

#### Technical Notes: 
1,650 m runway; domestic and charter
