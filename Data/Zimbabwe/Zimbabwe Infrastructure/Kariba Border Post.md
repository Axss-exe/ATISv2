# Kariba Border Post

**Infrastructure Type:**  Border Crossing
**Operational Status:** `operational`
**Operated By**: [[Zimbabwe Revenue Authority (ZIMRA)]] ; [[Zambia Revenue Authority (ZRA)]]
**Value Is**: A border crossing on Lake Kariba at the Zambezi River, providing access to Zambia's Siavonga district and the southern Copperbelt. Its existence offers an alternative route for copperbelt mining logistics and tourism between Zimbabwe and Zambia. Without it, the Kariba region would be isolated from Zambian supply chains, and the Siavonga-Kariba tourism circuit would be broken. The border operates limited hours and depends on ferry service, restricting commercial traffic.


## Summary

A border crossing on Lake Kariba at the Zambezi River, providing access to Zambia's Siavonga district and the southern Copperbelt. Its existence offers an alternative route for copperbelt mining logistics and tourism between Zimbabwe and Zambia. Without it, the Kariba region would be isolated from Zambian supply chains, and the Siavonga-Kariba tourism circuit would be broken. The border operates limited hours and depends on ferry service, restricting commercial traffic.
