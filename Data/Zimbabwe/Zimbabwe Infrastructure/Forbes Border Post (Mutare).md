# Forbes Border Post (Mutare)

**Infrastructure Type:**  Border Crossing
**Operational Status:** `operational / under_redevelopment`
**Operated By**: [[Zimbabwe Revenue Authority (ZRA)]]
**Value Is**: The eastern border with Mozambique, linking to the Machipanda rail line and Beira port (600 km). Its existence provides Zimbabwe's shortest maritime access for bulk mineral exports, critical for chrome and nickel competitiveness in Asian markets. Without it, all eastern exports must transit South Africa at 2x the distance and cost. A concession for redevelopment was awarded to Forbes Border Consortium in January 2025, but implementation timelines are uncertain. The surrounding area still contains landmine fields from the liberation war, restricting expansion.


## Summary

The eastern border with Mozambique, linking to the Machipanda rail line and Beira port (600 km). Its existence provides Zimbabwe's shortest maritime access for bulk mineral exports, critical for chrome and nickel competitiveness in Asian markets. Without it, all eastern exports must transit South Africa at 2x the distance and cost. A concession for redevelopment was awarded to Forbes Border Consortium in January 2025, but implementation timelines are uncertain. The surrounding area still contains landmine fields from the liberation war, restricting expansion.

#### Technical Notes: 
Gateway to Beira port; increasing commercial traffic
