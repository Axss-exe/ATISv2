# Port of Richards Bay (South Africa — Coal/Mineral Export)

**Infrastructure Type:**  Seaport
**Operational Status:** `operational`
**Operated By**: [[Transnet National Ports Authority]]
**Value Is**: The world's largest coal export terminal and a potential bulk mineral export hub for Zimbabwe. Its existence offers a dedicated coal export channel for Hwange thermal coal and a deep-water alternative for chrome and mineral sands. Without Richards Bay, Zimbabwe coal would have no viable seaborne export route (Beira lacks coal terminal capacity). The 1,200+ km distance and Transnet rail constraints make it economically marginal for Zimbabwe coal without significant rail investment.


## Summary

The world's largest coal export terminal and a potential bulk mineral export hub for Zimbabwe. Its existence offers a dedicated coal export channel for Hwange thermal coal and a deep-water alternative for chrome and mineral sands. Without Richards Bay, Zimbabwe coal would have no viable seaborne export route (Beira lacks coal terminal capacity). The 1,200+ km distance and Transnet rail constraints make it economically marginal for Zimbabwe coal without significant rail investment.
