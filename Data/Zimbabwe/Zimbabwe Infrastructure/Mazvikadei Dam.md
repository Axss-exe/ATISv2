# Mazvikadei Dam

**Infrastructure Type:**  Dam
**Operational Status:** `operational`
**Operated By**: [[Zimbabwe National Water Authority (ZINWA)]]
**Value Is**: A multi-purpose dam on the Manyame River supplying water to Chegutu and surrounding mining and agricultural areas. Its existence provides the only reliable water source for Chegutu's gold mines and the broader Mashonaland West mining belt. Without it, shallow groundwater depletion would force mine closures and agricultural collapse. The dam's catchment is increasingly polluted by upstream agricultural runoff, threatening water quality for industrial users.


## Summary

A multi-purpose dam on the Manyame River supplying water to Chegutu and surrounding mining and agricultural areas. Its existence provides the only reliable water source for Chegutu's gold mines and the broader Mashonaland West mining belt. Without it, shallow groundwater depletion would force mine closures and agricultural collapse. The dam's catchment is increasingly polluted by upstream agricultural runoff, threatening water quality for industrial users.

#### Technical Notes: 
300 million m³ storage
