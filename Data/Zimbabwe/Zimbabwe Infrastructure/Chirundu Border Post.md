# Chirundu Border Post

**Infrastructure Type:**  Border Crossing
**Operational Status:** `operational`
**Operated By**: [[Zimbabwe Revenue Authority (ZIMRA)]] ; [[Zambia Revenue Authority (ZRA)]]
**Value Is**: The Zambezi River crossing to Zambia, Africa's first One Stop Border Post (OSBP) since 2009. Its existence enables copper concentrate transit from the Zambian Copperbelt to South African ports, cobalt logistics, and mining equipment delivery to the DRC. Without it, Copperbelt mining would face a 400 km detour via Livingstone or Kazungula, adding 2 days and \$2,000+ per truck. The OSBP model has reduced crossing times from 2 days to 2–6 hours, but bridge capacity and seasonal flooding remain constraints.


## Summary

The Zambezi River crossing to Zambia, Africa's first One Stop Border Post (OSBP) since 2009. Its existence enables copper concentrate transit from the Zambian Copperbelt to South African ports, cobalt logistics, and mining equipment delivery to the DRC. Without it, Copperbelt mining would face a 400 km detour via Livingstone or Kazungula, adding 2 days and \$2,000+ per truck. The OSBP model has reduced crossing times from 2 days to 2–6 hours, but bridge capacity and seasonal flooding remain constraints.

#### Technical Notes: 
One Stop Border Post (OSBP) with Zambia; 24-hour operation
