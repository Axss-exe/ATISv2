# Hwange–Power Station Water Pipeline

**Infrastructure Type:**  Water Pipeline
**Operational Status:** `operational`
**Operated By**: [[Zimbabwe National Water Authority (ZINWA)]] ; [[Zimbabwe Power Company (ZPC)]]
**Value Is**: A dedicated pipeline from the Zambezi River to Hwange power station, providing the cooling water essential for thermal power generation. Its existence prevents the power station from shutting down during dry seasons when local water sources are depleted. Without it, Hwange would be limited to wet-season operation, and Zimbabwe's grid would lose 30–40% of dependable capacity year-round. The pipeline's pumps are electricity-dependent, creating a circular vulnerability during grid failures.


## Summary

A dedicated pipeline from the Zambezi River to Hwange power station, providing the cooling water essential for thermal power generation. Its existence prevents the power station from shutting down during dry seasons when local water sources are depleted. Without it, Hwange would be limited to wet-season operation, and Zimbabwe's grid would lose 30–40% of dependable capacity year-round. The pipeline's pumps are electricity-dependent, creating a circular vulnerability during grid failures.

#### Technical Notes: 
~50 km; supplies Hwange Thermal Power Station
