# Theotechnic College

## Summary
Theotechnic College uniquely empowers pastoral leaders by merging theological training with entrepreneurial skill development. Founded by [[Peter Makiriyado]], the college aims for graduates who are spiritually grounded and economically self-reliant contributors to rural [[Zimbabwe]] communities. Its mission is to equip pastors with the knowledge, skills, and resources to serve and fulfill the Great Commission holistically, effectively, and sustainably.
