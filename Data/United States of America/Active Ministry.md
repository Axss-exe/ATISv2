# ACTIVE Ministry

## Summary
ACTIVE Ministry is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization formed to facilitate the starting of new congregations in Zimbabwe and other countries in Africa. ACTIVE stands for Africa Church-planting & Training In Vocational Education. The ministry is establishing a theological and vocational training college (Theotechnic College) through which it trains and equips preachers to plant churches and be self-supported. It provides comprehensive support to Theotechnic College including administrative oversight, funding, and strategic planning.
