# Civil Aviation Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Civil Aviation Authority of Zimbabwe (CAAZ)]]"


## Description
This law establishes the Civil Aviation Authority of Zimbabwe (CAAZ) as the regulatory body for civil aviation. It was created to regulate civil aviation, ensure safety and security in air transport, and align with international aviation standards. It regulates aviation, transport, tourism, and logistics sectors. Investors should care because it is responsible for air navigation services, aircraft registration, personnel licensing, and aviation safety oversight, and provides for the establishment of the Airports Company of Zimbabwe (ACZ) to manage airport infrastructure.
