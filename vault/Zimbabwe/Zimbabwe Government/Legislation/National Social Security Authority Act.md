# National Social Security Authority Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[National Social Security Authority (NSSA)]]"


## Description
This law establishes the National Social Security Authority (NSSA) to administer social security schemes. It was created to protect workers through pension benefits, accident compensation, and social safety nets. It regulates all sectors with employees through the Pension and Other Benefits Scheme and the Accident Prevention and Workers Compensation Scheme. Investors should care because it mandates employer registration and monthly contributions, making it a core compliance obligation for all businesses with employees and affecting labor costs.
