# Zimbabwe Council for Higher Education Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Zimbabwe Council for Higher Education (ZIMCHE)]]"
  - "[[Ministry of Higher and Tertiary Education, Innovation, Science and Technology Development]]"


## Description
This law establishes ZIMCHE as the autonomous regulatory body for higher education. It was created to ensure quality standards in universities and colleges, align qualifications with national development needs, and protect students and employers from substandard education. It regulates higher education, research, technology transfer, and institutional partnerships. Investors should care because it governs the registration and accreditation of universities, polytechnics, and teacher training colleges, and assesses foreign qualifications, making it essential for foreign education providers and institutional partnerships.
