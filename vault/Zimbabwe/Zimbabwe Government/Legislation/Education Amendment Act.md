# Education Amendment Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education]]"
  - "[[Ministry of Higher and Tertiary Education, Innovation, Science and Technology Development]]"


## Description
This law amends the principal Education Act to introduce modern provisions including free and compulsory basic education, the prohibition of corporal punishment in schools, and the establishment of school development associations. It was created to improve school governance, ensure access to basic education, and modernize the education regulatory framework. It regulates education, private schools, corporate social responsibility, and curriculum development sectors. Investors should care because it strengthens the regulatory framework for private schools, curriculum governance, and the administration of public examinations, affecting all educational institutions and CSR education projects.
