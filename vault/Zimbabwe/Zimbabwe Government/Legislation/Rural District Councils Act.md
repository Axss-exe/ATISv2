# Rural District Councils Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Local Government and Public Works]]"
  - "[[ATIS/Zimbabwe/Zimbabwe Businesses/Companies/Regulatory Agencies/Rural District Councils]]"


## Description
This law establishes Rural District Councils as local authorities for rural areas. It was created to decentralize governance, empower rural communities, and manage rural infrastructure and natural resources. It regulates rural development, agriculture, mining, and community services sectors. Investors should care because it empowers councils to manage communal land, levy rates, provide local infrastructure, and regulate rural business operations, forming the foundational law for rural governance and natural resource management at the local level.
