# Electricity Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Zimbabwe Energy Regulatory Authority (ZERA)]]"
  - "[[Zimbabwe Electricity Supply Authority (ZESA)]]"


## Description
This law governs the generation, transmission, and supply of electricity. It was created to ensure reliable electricity supply, regulate the power sector, and facilitate private sector participation in energy generation. It regulates energy, electricity, mining, and manufacturing sectors. Investors should care because it acts as the legal backbone for Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs) between private Independent Power Producers (IPPs) and the state grid, enabling renewable energy and power infrastructure investments.
