# Zimbabwe Mining Development Corporation Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Zimbabwe Mining Development Corporation (ZMDC)]]"


## Description
This law establishes the Zimbabwe Mining Development Corporation (ZMDC) as a state-owned enterprise with the mandate to engage in mining and mineral beneficiation ventures. It was created to enable the state to participate directly in the mining sector and enter into strategic partnerships. It regulates mining, mineral beneficiation, and state-owned enterprise sectors. Investors should care because ZMDC has the power to enter into joint ventures, acquire mining rights, and operate as a commercial entity in the extractives sector, often partnering with foreign investors on strategic mineral projects.
