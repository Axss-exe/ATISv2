# Plant Pests and Diseases Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Lands, Agriculture, Water, Climate and Rural Resettlement]]"
  - "[[Chief Plant Protection Officer]]"


## Description
This law regulates the importation of plants, plant products, seeds, and growing media. It was created to prevent the introduction of foreign pests and diseases that could devastate local agriculture and biodiversity. It regulates agriculture, horticulture, forestry, import, and export sectors. Investors should care because it mandates import permits, phytosanitary certificates, and quarantine requirements, and prohibits the import of soil and certain plants from Asia and the Pacific Islands without special consent, critical for agricultural and horticultural investors importing vegetative material.
