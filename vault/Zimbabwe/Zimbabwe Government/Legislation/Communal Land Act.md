# Communal Land Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Lands and Rural Development]]"
  - "[[ATIS/Zimbabwe/Zimbabwe Businesses/Companies/Regulatory Agencies/Rural District Councils]]"


## Description
This law governs the administration and allocation of communal land, which is vested in the President and administered by Rural District Councils. It was created to ensure equitable access to land in rural areas, prevent land conflicts, and regulate land use in communal areas. It regulates agriculture, rural development, community projects, and livestock sectors. Investors should care because investors in rural agriculture or community projects must navigate this framework for land access, residential stands, and grazing rights.
