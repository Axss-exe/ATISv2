# Apprenticeship Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Higher and Tertiary Education, Innovation, Science and Technology Development]]"
  - "[[National Manpower Advisory Council]]"


## Description
This law regulates the training of apprentices in designated trades. It was created to build a skilled artisan workforce, ensure standardized technical training, and address skills gaps in industrial sectors. It regulates manufacturing, construction, mining, engineering, and technical skills sectors. Investors should care because employers in manufacturing, construction, mining, and engineering must comply with its provisions when training artisanal staff, and it underpins the technical skills pipeline for industrial sectors, affecting labor quality and training costs.
