# Zimbabwe National Water Authority Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Zimbabwe National Water Authority (ZINWA)]]"


## Description
This law establishes the Zimbabwe National Water Authority (ZINWA) as the statutory body responsible for water resources management and supply infrastructure. It was created to centralize water resource management, ensure equitable distribution, and regulate bulk water supply. It regulates water, agriculture, mining, manufacturing, and urban development sectors. Investors should care because ZINWA fixes water charges, enters into joint ventures for water infrastructure, and regulates bulk water supply, making it critical for agricultural and industrial water security and project viability.
