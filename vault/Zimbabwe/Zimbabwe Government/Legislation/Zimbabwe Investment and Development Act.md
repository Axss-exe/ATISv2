# Zimbabwe Investment and Development Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Zimbabwe Investment and Development Agency (ZIDA)]]"


## Description
This law is the cornerstone of Zimbabwe's foreign direct investment framework, enacted in 2019. It was created to streamline bureaucratic bottlenecks by establishing ZIDA as a One-Stop Investment Services Centre, consolidating and repealing three previous laws. It regulates all sectors open to foreign investment, particularly manufacturing, mining, energy, and services. Investors should care because it provides legal guarantees against state expropriation, protected rights to repatriate profits and dividends, and established frameworks for international dispute resolution.
