# Patents Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Zimbabwe Intellectual Property Office (ZIPO)]]"
  - "[[Ministry of Justice, Legal and Parliamentary Affairs]]"


## Description
This law provides for the registration and protection of patents for inventions. It was created to incentivize innovation, protect intellectual property rights, and promote technological development. It regulates technology, pharmaceuticals, manufacturing, research and development, and innovation sectors. Investors should care because it governs the patent application process, including specification requirements, claims, and priority claims under the Paris Convention, protecting intellectual property rights for foreign and domestic inventors and technology investors.
