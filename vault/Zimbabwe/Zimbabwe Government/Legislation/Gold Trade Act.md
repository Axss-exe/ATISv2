# Gold Trade Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Fidelity Gold Refiners (Pvt) Ltd]]"
  - "[[Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe (RBZ)]]"
  - "[[ATIS/Zimbabwe/Zimbabwe Government/Ministries/Ministry of Mines and Mining Development]]"


## Description
This law prohibits unauthorized possession and dealing in gold, establishing a state-controlled trading framework. It was created to prevent gold smuggling, ensure state revenue from gold exports, and maintain control over the precious metals trade. It regulates mining, gold trading, precious metals, and finance sectors. Investors should care because Fidelity Gold Refiners holds the monopoly on gold buying, refining, and export, and all gold exporters must obtain a gold dealing license and comply with RBZ export procedures.
