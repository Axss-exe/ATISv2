# Postal and Telecommunications Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Postal and Telecommunications Regulatory Authority (POTRAZ)]]"


## Description
This law outlines corporate licensing regimes for internet service providers and telecommunications companies. It was created to regulate the postal and telecommunications sector, ensure national security in communications, and manage scarce spectrum resources. It regulates telecommunications, internet, postal services, and technology sectors. Investors should care because it governs data interception laws, national spectrum allocations, and licensing requirements for telecom and ISP operations, affecting market entry and operational compliance.
