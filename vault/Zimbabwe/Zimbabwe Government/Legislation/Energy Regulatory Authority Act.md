# Energy Regulatory Authority Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Zimbabwe Energy Regulatory Authority (ZERA)]]"


## Description
This law establishes the Zimbabwe Energy Regulatory Authority (ZERA) to regulate the electricity and petroleum sectors. It was created to ensure reliable energy supply, consumer protection, and orderly development of the energy market. It regulates energy, electricity, petroleum, and renewable energy sectors. Investors should care because it handles Independent Power Producer (IPP) licensing frameworks and infrastructure tariff approval mechanisms, which are critical for investors building off-grid or captive solar operations and energy infrastructure projects.
