# Grain Marketing Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Grain Marketing Board (GMB)]]"
  - "[[Ministry of Lands, Agriculture, Water, Climate and Rural Resettlement]]"


## Description
This law regulates the marketing of grain and oilseeds in Zimbabwe. It was created to ensure food security, stabilize grain prices, regulate the grain trade, and maintain strategic grain reserves. It regulates agriculture, grain trading, food processing, milling, import, and export sectors. Investors should care because it establishes the Grain Marketing Board (GMB) as the buyer of last resort, governs the licensing of private grain traders, silo operators, and millers, and provides for strategic grain reserves and the regulation of grain imports and exports to ensure food security.
