# Census and Statistics Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Zimbabwe National Statistics Agency (ZIMSTAT)]]"


## Description
This law establishes the Zimbabwe National Statistics Agency (ZIMSTAT) as the central agency for official statistics. It was created to centralize official statistics collection, ensure data integrity, and provide evidence for national planning and policy formulation. It regulates all sectors through economic planning, research, and policy formulation datasets. Investors should care because it governs the conduct of national censuses, surveys, and statistical data collection, and provides the legal basis for economic planning and investment decision-making datasets.
