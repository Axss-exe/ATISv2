# Value Added Tax Regulations

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Zimbabwe Revenue Authority (ZIMRA)]]"


## Description
This law controls corporate standard VAT filings, import VAT exclusions, and zero-rated categories. It was created to establish a consumption tax system and ensure revenue from goods and services transactions. It regulates all sectors, particularly trade, manufacturing, and retail. Investors should care because it affects short-term liquidity management on imported capital mining equipment through zero-rated categories and VAT refund mechanisms, impacting cash flow and working capital requirements.
