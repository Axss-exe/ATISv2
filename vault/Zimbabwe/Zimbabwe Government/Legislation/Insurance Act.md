# Insurance Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Insurance and Pensions Commission (IPEC)]]"


## Description
This law governs the licensing, regulation, and supervision of insurance companies, brokers, and agents. It was created to ensure the stability of the insurance sector, protect policyholders, and maintain market confidence. It regulates insurance, finance, risk management, and corporate services sectors. Investors should care because it establishes solvency requirements, policyholder protection frameworks, and reinsurance rules, ensuring compliance for corporate risk management programs and insurance market entry.
