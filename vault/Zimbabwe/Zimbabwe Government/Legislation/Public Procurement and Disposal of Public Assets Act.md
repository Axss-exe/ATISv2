# Public Procurement and Disposal of Public Assets Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Procurement Regulatory Authority of Zimbabwe (PRAZ)]]"


## Description
This law governs public procurement processes, contract awards, and the disposal of state assets. It was created to ensure transparency, competition, and value for money in government spending, and to prevent corruption in public contracting. It regulates government contracting, construction, services, and public-private partnership sectors. Investors should care because it mandates competitive bidding, transparency in tender awards, and sets thresholds for various procurement methods, essential for any entity seeking government contracts or public-private partnerships.
