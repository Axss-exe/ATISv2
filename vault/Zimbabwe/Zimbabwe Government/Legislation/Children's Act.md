# Children's Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Public Service, Labour and Social Welfare]]"
  - "[[Department of Social Services]]"


## Description
This law provides for the care, protection, and welfare of children. It was created to safeguard children's rights, regulate institutions caring for minors, and prevent child abuse, neglect, and exploitation. It regulates education, social services, child welfare, and corporate social responsibility sectors. Investors should care because it mandates compliance when operating corporate social responsibility school infrastructures, boarding facilities, or any institution caring for minors, and governs adoption procedures, child custody, and the prohibition of child assault and abandonment.
