# Pre-Shipment Inspection and Verification of Imports Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Zimbabwe Revenue Authority (ZIMRA)]]"
  - "[[Ministry of Industry and Commerce]]"
  - "[[Standards Association of Zimbabwe (SAZ)]]"


## Description
This law mandates the pre-shipment inspection and verification of imports to ensure conformity with Zimbabwean standards, correct valuation, and proper classification. It was created to prevent dumping, combat under-invoicing, and ensure the importation of quality goods that meet national standards. It regulates import, manufacturing, trade, construction, automotive, and consumer goods sectors. Investors should care because it requires that designated imports be inspected in the country of origin by authorized inspection agencies before shipment, aiming to prevent the importation of substandard goods and affecting all major import categories.
