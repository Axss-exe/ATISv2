# Money Laundering and Proceeds of Crime Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU)]]"
  - "[[Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe (RBZ)]]"
  - "[[Zimbabwe Revenue Authority (ZIMRA)]]"
  - "[[National Prosecuting Authority]]"


## Description
This law criminalizes money laundering, terrorist financing, and the concealment of proceeds of crime. It was created to combat financial crime, align with international anti-money laundering standards, and protect the integrity of the financial system. It regulates banking, finance, mining, real estate, trade, and legal services sectors. Investors should care because it mandates know-your-customer (KYC), suspicious transaction reporting, and record-keeping for financial institutions and designated non-financial businesses, and governs cross-border currency disclosure requirements exceeding US$15,000.
