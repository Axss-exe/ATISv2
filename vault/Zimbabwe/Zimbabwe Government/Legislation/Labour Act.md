# Labour Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Public Service, Labour and Social Welfare]]"
  - "[[National Employment Councils (NECs)]]"


## Description
This law is the primary legislation governing employment relations, collective bargaining, and workers' rights. It was created to protect workers, regulate industrial relations, and establish fair labor standards across industries. It regulates all sectors with employees, particularly manufacturing, mining, and industrial relations. Investors should care because it establishes National Employment Councils by industry, regulates minimum wages, unfair dismissal procedures, and retrenchment protocols, making it the cornerstone of industrial relations compliance for all employers.
