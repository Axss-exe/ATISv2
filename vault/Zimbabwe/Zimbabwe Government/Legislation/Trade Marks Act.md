# Trade Marks Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Zimbabwe Intellectual Property Office (ZIPO)]]"
  - "[[Ministry of Justice, Legal and Parliamentary Affairs]]"


## Description
This law provides for the registration, protection, and enforcement of trade marks, service marks, and collective marks. It was created to protect brand identity, commercial goodwill, and consumer interests from counterfeit and misleading branding. It regulates consumer goods, retail, franchising, manufacturing, and brand management sectors. Investors should care because it governs the application process, opposition procedures, infringement remedies, and the renewal of marks, protecting brand identity and commercial goodwill for local and foreign businesses.
