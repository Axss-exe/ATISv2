# Deeds Registries Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Registrar of Deeds]]"
  - "[[Ministry of Justice, Legal and Parliamentary Affairs]]"


## Description
This law governs the registration of immovable property transactions, including transfers, bonds, and notarial deeds. It was created to provide legal certainty for land and property ownership records and prevent fraudulent property transactions. It regulates real estate, property development, banking, and construction sectors. Investors should care because it provides the legal certainty for land and property ownership records, essential for real estate transactions, mortgage registrations, and property-backed investment security.
