# Environmental Management Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Environmental Management Agency (EMA)]]"


## Description
This law governs the sustainable management of natural resources and environmental protection. It was created to prevent environmental degradation and ensure sustainable development across industrial activities. It regulates mining, infrastructure, manufacturing, agriculture, and energy sectors. Investors should care because it mandates obtaining an Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) certificate prior to project commencement, and defines waste management standards and pollution control limits that directly affect operational compliance costs and project timelines.
