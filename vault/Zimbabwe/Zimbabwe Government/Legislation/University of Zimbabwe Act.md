# University of Zimbabwe Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[University of Zimbabwe]]"
  - "[[Ministry of Higher and Tertiary Education, Innovation, Science and Technology Development]]"


## Description
This law incorporates the University of Zimbabwe as the nation's flagship public university and establishes its governance structure. It was created to establish a center of excellence for higher education and research, and to ensure academic freedom and institutional autonomy. It regulates higher education, research, technology transfer, and academic partnerships. Investors should care because it governs the granting of degrees, research mandates, and institutional autonomy, making it relevant for academic joint ventures, research partnerships, and technology transfer agreements.
