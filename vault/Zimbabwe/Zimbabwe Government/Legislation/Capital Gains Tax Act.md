# Capital Gains Tax Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Zimbabwe Revenue Authority (ZIMRA)]]"


## Description
This law governs tax liability calculations on the disposal of specified capital assets. It was created to tax capital appreciation and ensure government revenue from asset transfers and property sales. It regulates real estate, securities, corporate finance, and mergers and acquisitions sectors. Investors should care because it dictates corporate M&A restructuring costs and tax obligations on the sale of real estate or marketable securities, affecting transaction structuring and net returns.
