# Tobacco Marketing and Levy Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Tobacco Industry and Marketing Board (TIMB)]]"
  - "[[Ministry of Lands, Agriculture, Water, Climate and Rural Resettlement]]"


## Description
This law provides for the control and regulation of the tobacco industry, including the growing, marketing, and exporting of tobacco. It was created to ensure orderly marketing, quality control, and revenue collection from Zimbabwe's primary agricultural export. It regulates tobacco, agriculture, export, manufacturing, and agribusiness sectors. Investors should care because it establishes the Tobacco Industry and Marketing Board (TIMB) to register growers, license buyers and auction floors, regulate contract farming, and impose levies on tobacco grown or sold, also controlling the use of agricultural remedies on tobacco lands.
