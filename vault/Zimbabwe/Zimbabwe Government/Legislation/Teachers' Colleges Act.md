# Teachers' Colleges Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education]]"
  - "[[Ministry of Higher and Tertiary Education, Innovation, Science and Technology Development]]"


## Description
This law provides for the establishment, governance, and regulation of teachers' colleges. It was created to ensure quality teacher training, standardize educator certification, and maintain adequate supply of qualified teachers. It regulates education, teacher training, and curriculum development sectors. Investors should care because it governs the accreditation of teacher training programs, the certification of educators, and the standards for pedagogical training, affecting corporate training providers and any institution offering educator development programs.
