# Income Tax Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Zimbabwe Revenue Authority (ZIMRA)]]"


## Description
This law is the primary legislation shaping corporate tax obligations in Zimbabwe. It was created to establish the income tax framework and ensure government revenue from business profits, employment income, and investment returns. It regulates all sectors through corporate tax brackets, allowable expenses, and dividend withholding tax. Investors should care because it sets transfer pricing thresholds for multi-jurisdictional investments, dividend withholding tax percentages, and allowable deductions, directly affecting net profitability and tax planning.
