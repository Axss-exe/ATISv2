# National Archives of Zimbabwe Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Home Affairs and Cultural Heritage]]"
  - "[[National Archives of Zimbabwe]]"
  - "[[Director of National Archives]]"


## Description
This law, Chapter 25:06, provides for the storage, preservation, and management of public archives and public records. It was created to safeguard the national documentary heritage, ensure proper record-keeping by government and statutory bodies, and regulate access to historical records. It regulates government administration, research, media, publishing, and digital preservation sectors. Investors should care because it mandates the preservation of public records, controls the reproduction and publication of archival materials, prohibits the removal of public archives from Zimbabwe without official purpose, and establishes protected historical records, affecting businesses involved in government contracting, research, publishing, and any entity creating records subject to archival preservation.
