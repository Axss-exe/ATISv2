# Parks and Wildlife Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Zimbabwe Parks and Wildlife Management Authority (ZIMPARKS)]]"


## Description
This law governs the establishment and management of national parks, safari areas, botanical reserves, and sanctuaries. It was created to conserve wildlife, regulate sustainable use of natural resources, and promote eco-tourism. It regulates tourism, wildlife conservation, hospitality, and eco-tourism sectors. Investors should care because it regulates hunting permits, trophy trade, and tourism concessions, which are critical for eco-tourism and hospitality investors operating near or within protected areas.
