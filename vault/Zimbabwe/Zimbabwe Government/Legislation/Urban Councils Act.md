# Urban Councils Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Local Government and Public Works]]"
  - "[[Urban Councils]]"


## Description
This law provides for the establishment, powers, and functions of urban municipalities (city, town, and local councils). It was created to ensure orderly urban development, efficient local service delivery, and municipal governance. It regulates urban development, real estate, construction, retail, and manufacturing sectors. Investors should care because it governs municipal planning, building approvals, water and sanitation services, road maintenance, and local business licensing, making it the primary local governance framework affecting urban commercial and real estate development.
