# Indigenisation and Economic Empowerment Act

**Status**: Active - Amended
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[National Indigenisation and Economic Empowerment Unit]]"
  - "[[Ministry of Women Affairs, Community, Small and Medium Enterprises Development]]"


## Description
This law provides for the economic empowerment of indigenous Zimbabweans through support measures and the establishment of the National Indigenisation and Economic Empowerment Board. It was created to ensure indigenous participation in the economy and redistribute economic control. It regulates mining, banking, manufacturing, and other designated sectors. Investors should care because while the mandatory 51% indigenous ownership requirement has been relaxed for most sectors, it remains applicable to designated extractive industries and reserved economic sectors, affecting ownership structures.
