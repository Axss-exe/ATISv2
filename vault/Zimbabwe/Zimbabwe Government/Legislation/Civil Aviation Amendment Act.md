# Civil Aviation Amendment Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Civil Aviation Authority of Zimbabwe (CAAZ)]]"
  - "[[Airports Company of Zimbabwe (ACZ)]]"


## Description
This law amends the Civil Aviation Act to provide for the unbundling of CAAZ into a regulatory authority and a separate airport operator (ACZ). It was created to align with ICAO standards by separating regulatory and operational functions. It regulates aviation, transport, and airport infrastructure sectors. Investors should care because it transfers airport assets and operations to ACZ while CAAZ retains safety, security, and air navigation functions, affecting airport concession opportunities and aviation investment frameworks.
