# Microfinance Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe (RBZ)]]"


## Description
This law regulates the establishment and operation of microfinance institutions. It was created to promote financial inclusion, provide alternative lending channels for underserved populations, and regulate informal credit markets. It regulates microfinance, banking, SME finance, and rural development sectors. Investors should care because it provides for licensing tiers (credit-only vs. deposit-taking), consumer protection, interest rate disclosures, and reporting requirements, supporting financial inclusion and alternative lending channels for SMEs and rural entrepreneurs.
