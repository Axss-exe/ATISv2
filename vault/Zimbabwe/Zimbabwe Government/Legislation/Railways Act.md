# Railways Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[National Railways of Zimbabwe (NRZ)]]"
  - "[[Ministry of Transport and Infrastructural Development]]"


## Description
This law provides for the construction, operation, and regulation of railway infrastructure. It was created to regulate rail transport, ensure safe and efficient freight movement, and facilitate bulk commodity logistics. It regulates transport, logistics, mining, and bulk commodity sectors. Investors should care because it governs the powers of NRZ as the state railway operator, including land acquisition for rail lines, safety standards, and freight tariffs, relevant for logistics, mining, and bulk commodity transport investors.
