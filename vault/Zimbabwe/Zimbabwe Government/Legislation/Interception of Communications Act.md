# Interception of Communications Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Transport and Infrastructural Development]]"
  - "[[Monitoring of Interception of Communications Centre (MICC)]]"
  - "[[Zimbabwe Republic Police (ZRP)]]"


## Description
This law, Chapter 11:20, establishes the framework for lawful interception of postal, telecommunication, and electronic communications. It was created to balance national security interests with privacy rights, providing legal procedures for surveillance while preventing unauthorized interception. It regulates telecommunications, internet services, postal services, banking, and security sectors. Investors should care because service providers must establish interception interfaces, comply with warrant-based surveillance requests, and face penalties for unauthorized interception, making it critical for telecom operators, ISPs, and any business handling sensitive communications to understand their obligations and liabilities under state surveillance powers.
