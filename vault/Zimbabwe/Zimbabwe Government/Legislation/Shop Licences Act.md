# Shop Licences Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Industry and Commerce]]"
  - "[[Local Authorities]]"


## Description
This law governs the licensing of retail and wholesale trading establishments. It was created to regulate commercial trading, ensure orderly retail development, and maintain public health and safety standards in trading premises. It regulates retail, wholesale, commerce, and food and beverage sectors. Investors should care because all shops, supermarkets, and trading premises must obtain annual licenses from local authorities, and it regulates trading hours, hygiene standards, and zoning compliance for commercial operations.
