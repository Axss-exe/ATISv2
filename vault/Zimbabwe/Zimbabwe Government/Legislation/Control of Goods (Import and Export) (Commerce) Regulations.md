# Control of Goods (Import and Export) (Commerce) Regulations

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Industry and Commerce]]"
  - "[[Zimbabwe Revenue Authority (ZIMRA)]]"


## Description
This law is the statutory instrument framework governing the licensing of controlled imports and exports. It was created to protect local industry from dumping, preserve foreign currency, and align with the National Development Strategy 2 (NDS2). It regulates import, export, manufacturing, retail, agriculture, and construction sectors. Investors should care because it requires specific import licenses for designated goods including cement, steel, textiles, footwear, and pasta, moving away from the Open General Import License model and adding compliance layers to import operations.
