# Revenue Authority Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Zimbabwe Revenue Authority (ZIMRA)]]"


## Description
This law establishes the Zimbabwe Revenue Authority (ZIMRA) as the principal agency responsible for collecting national revenue and enforcing tax and customs laws. It was created to consolidate tax and customs administration under a single authority to improve revenue collection efficiency. It regulates all sectors through corporate tax obligations, audits, and border customs clearances. Investors should care because it provides the statutory backend for all tax compliance, customs clearances, and revenue enforcement that directly affects operational costs, cash flow, and import/export timelines.
