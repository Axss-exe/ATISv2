# Medicines and Allied Substances Control Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Medicines Control Authority of Zimbabwe (MCAZ)]]"
  - "[[Ministry of Health and Child Care]]"


## Description
This law establishes the Medicines Control Authority of Zimbabwe (MCAZ) to regulate the registration, importation, manufacture, and distribution of medicines, veterinary medicines, and medical devices. It was created to ensure the safety, quality, and efficacy of medicines available in Zimbabwe and protect public health. It regulates pharmaceuticals, healthcare, veterinary medicine, import, export, and medical devices sectors. Investors should care because it requires licensing of pharmaceutical premises and persons, clinical trial approvals, and the registration of all medicines before sale, and governs the import and export of unregistered medicines for special purposes.
