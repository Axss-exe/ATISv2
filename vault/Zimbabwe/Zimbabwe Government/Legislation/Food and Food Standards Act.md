# Food and Food Standards Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Health and Child Care]]"
  - "[[Environmental Health Department]]"
  - "[[Standards Association of Zimbabwe (SAZ)]]"


## Description
This law provides for the control and regulation of food hygiene, safety standards, and food labeling. It was created to protect public health, ensure consumer safety, and prevent foodborne illnesses and contamination. It regulates food manufacturing, food processing, import, export, retail, and hospitality sectors. Investors should care because it establishes standards for the composition, quality, and purity of food products, empowers health inspectors to examine food premises, seize unsafe food, and prosecute offenders, affecting all food manufacturers, processors, importers, and retailers.
