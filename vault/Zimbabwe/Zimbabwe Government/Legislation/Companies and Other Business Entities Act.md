# Companies and Other Business Entities Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Registrar of Companies]]"


## Description
This law modernized corporate governance in Zimbabwe, replacing the old Companies Act. It was created to align with international best practices, enhance transparency, and protect stakeholders in corporate entities. It regulates all sectors through corporate governance requirements. Investors should care because it introduces provisions for electronic registration, mandatory beneficial ownership tracking, and stricter protection for minority shareholders, reducing investment risk and improving corporate accountability.
