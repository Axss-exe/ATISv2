# Copyright and Neighbouring Rights Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Justice, Legal and Parliamentary Affairs]]"
  - "[[Zimbabwe Intellectual Property Office (ZIPO)]]"


## Description
This law, Chapter 26:05, provides for the protection of copyright in literary, artistic, musical works, computer programs, databases, and audio-visual productions. It was created to incentivize creativity, protect intellectual property rights, and align with international copyright conventions. It regulates publishing, music, film, software development, digital content, broadcasting, and e-commerce sectors. Investors should care because it governs the reproduction, distribution, broadcasting, and adaptation of copyrighted works, including computer programs and databases, establishes criminal penalties for circumvention of copy-protection devices, and regulates the import and export of infringing copies, essential for content creators, software developers, and digital platform operators.
