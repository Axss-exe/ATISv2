# Agricultural Marketing Authority Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Agricultural Marketing Authority (AMA)]]"


## Description
This law establishes the Agricultural Marketing Authority (AMA) to regulate the marketing of agricultural products. It was created to ensure food security, promote fair trade practices, and establish orderly agricultural markets. It regulates agriculture, agribusiness, food processing, and grain trading sectors. Investors should care because it governs the licensing of commodity buyers, grading standards, and the establishment of agricultural commodity exchanges, critical for agribusiness and food processing investors.
