# Technical and Vocational Education and Training Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Higher and Tertiary Education, Innovation, Science and Technology Development]]"
  - "[[Zimbabwe Council for Higher Education (ZIMCHE)]]"


## Description
This law governs the establishment and accreditation of technical and vocational education and training (TVET) institutions. It was created to align vocational qualifications with industry needs, build a skilled workforce, and promote competency-based education. It regulates technical education, vocational training, manufacturing, mining, and technology sectors. Investors should care because it provides for the development of competency-based curricula, skills assessment, and the alignment of vocational qualifications with industry needs, critical for investors in manufacturing, mining, and technology sectors requiring certified technical labor pools.
