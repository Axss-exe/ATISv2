# Disabled Persons Act

**Status**: Active
**Enforced By**: 
  - "[[Ministry of Public Service, Labour and Social Welfare]]"
  - "[[National Disability Board]]"


## Description
This law provides for the welfare, rehabilitation, and equal treatment of persons with disabilities. It was created to eliminate discrimination against persons with disabilities and ensure accessibility and inclusion in public life. It regulates employment, construction, transport, education, and public services sectors. Investors should care because it mandates accessibility standards in public buildings, transport, and educational institutions, and imposes obligations on employers and facility operators to ensure reasonable accommodation and non-discrimination in hiring, service provision, and infrastructure design.
