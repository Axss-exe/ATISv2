#### Function
The Ministry of Public Service, Labour and Social Welfare is mandated to promote equitable, efficient, and sustainable public service delivery, foster decent work conditions, and ensure comprehensive social protection for all citizens. The Ministry formulates and implements policies that regulate employment standards, occupational health and safety, and collective labour relations, while overseeing the recruitment, training, and management of civil servants. It administers social welfare programmes, including pensions, unemployment benefits, and assistance for vulnerable groups, coordinating with regional and local authorities to ensure uniform service coverage. The Ministry monitors compliance with labour legislation, conducts inspections, and adjudicates disputes through its labour tribunals. It collaborates with international partners and other government agencies to align national standards with global best practices, supports capacity building initiatives for the public service workforce, and conducts research to inform evidence-based reforms.
