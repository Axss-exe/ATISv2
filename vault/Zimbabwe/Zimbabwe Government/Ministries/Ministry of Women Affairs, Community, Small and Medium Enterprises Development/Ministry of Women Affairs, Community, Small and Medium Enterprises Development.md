#### Function
The Ministry of Women Affairs, Community, Small and Medium Enterprises Development is mandated to promote gender equality, empower women, strengthen community cohesion, and foster the growth and sustainability of small and medium enterprises as drivers of inclusive economic development. The Ministry's operations are organized around four core pillars. First, policy formulation and legislative advocacy focus on creating an enabling environment for women's participation in public and private sectors, and for SMEs to access finance, markets, and technology. Second, capacity-building programs deliver training, mentorship, and technical assistance to women entrepreneurs, community leaders, and SME owners, emphasizing skills such as financial management, digital literacy, and value-chain integration. Third, the Ministry administers grant and micro-credit schemes, collaborates with financial institutions, and coordinates public-private partnerships to improve resource mobilization and investment in targeted sectors. Fourth, monitoring, evaluation, and research units collect data, assess program impact, and disseminate best-practice guidelines to inform continuous improvement.
