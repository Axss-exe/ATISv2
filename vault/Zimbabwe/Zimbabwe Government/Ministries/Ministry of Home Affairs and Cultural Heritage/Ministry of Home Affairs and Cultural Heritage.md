#### Function
The Ministry of Home Affairs and Cultural Heritage is mandated to maintain a secure, accountable, and transparent environment that supports the development of a prosperous and globally competitive Zimbabwe. The Ministry delivers its mandate through operational activities coordinated across several departments and service units. The Department of Immigration manages entry, exit, and residency processes, while the Department of National Archives preserves historical records and cultural artifacts. The Central Registry oversees the registration of citizens and legal entities. The Ministry runs mobile registration exercises to facilitate nationwide citizen registration and identity verification. Its Media Centre disseminates information, and a dedicated dashboard offers real-time updates on news and departmental calendars. The Ministry liaises with the Zimbabwe Republic Police, collaborates with the Parliament of Zimbabwe, and provides online services through secure portals. The Ministry's website serves as a central hub for accessing resources, contacting officials, and navigating quick links to related government agencies.
