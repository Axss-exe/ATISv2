#### Function
The Ministry of Agriculture, Mechanisation and Water Resources Development is mandated to promote and sustain a viable agricultural sector through the provision of appropriate agricultural infrastructure, mechanisation, technical, administrative and advisory services in order to optimize agricultural productivity to ensure food security and economic development. The Ministry oversees crop production, livestock and veterinary services, agricultural mechanization, irrigation development, and research and specialist services. It coordinates agricultural extension services through AGRITEX, manages water resources for agricultural use, and implements policies that guide agricultural production and land use. The Ministry regulates agricultural practices, seed certification, and mechanization standards, and collaborates with research institutions and development partners to enhance the agricultural value chain. It monitors sector performance, provides evidence-based inputs for budgeting and resource mobilization, and supports rural livelihoods and national economic growth.
