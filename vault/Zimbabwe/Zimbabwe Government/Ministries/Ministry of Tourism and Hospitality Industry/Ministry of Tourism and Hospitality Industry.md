#### Function
The Ministry of Tourism and Hospitality Industry is mandated to develop, promote, and sustain Zimbabwe's tourism and hospitality sector by leveraging the country's natural, cultural, and heritage assets to generate economic growth, employment, and investment. The Ministry administers duty rebates and tax incentives in partnership with the Zimbabwe Revenue Authority to attract private investment, and oversees the licensing of tourism operators through the Zimbabwe Tourism Authority. Investment promotion activities target infrastructure development and the expansion of tourism offerings across a cluster-based framework that includes nature and wildlife, culture and heritage, MICE, township, rural, sports, medical, VFR, holiday and recreation, study, religious, and diplomatic tourism. The Ministry coordinates marketing campaigns, facilitates community-based tourism initiatives, and provides data to support planning. Through these coordinated efforts, the Ministry seeks to position Zimbabwe as a competitive, year-round destination for both leisure and business travelers.
