#### Function
The Ministry of Information, Publicity and Broadcasting Services is mandated to formulate and implement dynamic media and information policies that promote rights, national development and sovereignty. The Ministry administers information-related Acts for compliance, disseminates information to citizens on government policies and programmes, and articulates Zimbabwe's position nationally and internationally. It supports government ministries in developing information structures and promotes national culture, unity, identity and cohesion. The Ministry provides rural information services to bridge the information divide, facilitates fairs and exhibitions, and trains information and media personnel. It oversees the Zimbabwe Broadcasting Corporation, Broadcasting Authority of Zimbabwe, Zimpapers, and other government media institutions.
