#### Function
The Ministry of Justice, Legal and Parliamentary Affairs aims to uphold, develop and provide accessible, efficient and effective justice delivery. The Ministry's operations are organized into several directorates and programmes that together support the administration of justice. The Pre-Trial Diversion unit manages alternative dispute resolution for minor offences, while the Deeds, Companies and Intellectual Property directorate handles registration and regulation of property, corporate entities and intellectual property rights. Policy and Legal Research conducts legal studies and formulates policy recommendations, and the Law Development Commission oversees law making and reform. The Legal Aid Directorate offers legal advice and representation to indigent persons in civil and criminal matters. Constitutional and Parliamentary Affairs promotes public awareness of the Constitution. Community Service enforces court-ordered community service for non-serious offenders, and Zimbabwe Prisons and Correctional Services manage incarceration and rehabilitation of offenders, ensuring safe and humane control.
