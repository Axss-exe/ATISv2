#### Function
The Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education is dedicated to ensuring equitable, high-quality education for all children and adolescents, fostering their intellectual, social, and civic development in alignment with national goals. The Ministry formulates and implements policies governing curriculum design, assessment standards, teacher qualification, and school infrastructure across public and private primary and secondary institutions. It oversees the allocation of budgetary resources, monitors compliance with statutory regulations, and conducts periodic audits of school performance. The Ministry administers national examinations, accredits educational programs, and coordinates professional development initiatives for educators. It collaborates with regional education offices, local authorities, and stakeholder groups to address disparities, promote inclusive practices, and integrate technology into classroom instruction. Data collection and analysis inform evidence-based decision-making, while the Ministry provides guidance on health, safety, and welfare matters affecting students. Through these operational activities, the Ministry aims to maintain consistent educational standards, support continuous improvement, and prepare learners for further study and participation in the broader economy.
