#### Function
The Ministry of Veterans of the Liberation Struggle Affairs is tasked with preserving the legacy of the liberation struggle, safeguarding the rights and welfare of its veterans, and promoting national unity through remembrance and support initiatives. The Ministry administers a comprehensive benefits program that includes pension disbursement, healthcare access, and housing assistance for eligible veterans and their families. It maintains a centralized registry of liberation struggle participants, verifies eligibility, and coordinates with other government agencies to ensure timely delivery of entitlements. The Ministry oversees the allocation of commemorative funds for memorial sites, museums, and educational projects that document the historical contributions of veterans. It organizes annual remembrance ceremonies, public outreach events, and veteran-focused training workshops aimed at social reintegration and skill development. The Ministry conducts research on the socioeconomic status of veterans, publishes periodic reports, and advises the cabinet on policy reforms related to veteran affairs.
