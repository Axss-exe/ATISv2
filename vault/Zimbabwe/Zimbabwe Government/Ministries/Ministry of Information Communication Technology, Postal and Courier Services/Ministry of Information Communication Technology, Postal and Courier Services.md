#### Function
The Ministry of Information Communication Technology, Postal and Courier Services is mandated to develop, regulate and promote an integrated digital and postal ecosystem that enhances connectivity, facilitates efficient communication, and supports socioeconomic development across the nation. The Ministry formulates policies and regulatory frameworks for information and communication technology, overseeing the deployment of broadband infrastructure, spectrum management, and cybersecurity standards. It coordinates with public and private sector stakeholders to implement e-government services, digital literacy programs, and innovation initiatives that expand access to ICT resources. In the postal and courier domain, the Ministry supervises national postal operators, sets service quality benchmarks, and ensures compliance with international mailing conventions. It manages licensing, pricing, and performance monitoring for courier service providers, while promoting the adoption of automated sorting, tracking, and delivery technologies. The Ministry conducts research, provides technical assistance, and monitors market trends to align regulatory actions with emerging technologies.
