#### Function
The Ministry of Skills Audit and Development is mandated to assess, coordinate and enhance Zimbabwe's national skills ecosystem, aligning workforce capabilities with the country's socioeconomic priorities and fostering inclusive, innovation-driven growth. The Ministry's operations span a range of programmes and partnerships aimed at building practical competencies and digital capacity. It convenes inter-ministerial forums to map skills needs, supports provincial skills fairs that showcase vocational talent, and administers the Zimbabwe Global Skills Partnership Programme and its digital hub for international job placement. The Ministry formalises memoranda of understanding with technology entities to create skills development hubs, and collaborates with financial institutions and waste management firms to integrate financial literacy and green skill training. The Ministry coordinates national and regional stakeholder engagement, positioning Zimbabwe as a host for continental skills development initiatives.
