## ECD Centre Registration Certificate

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education (MoPSE)]]
**Processing Time**: 3 to 4 months
**Document Type**: Certificate


## Description
The **ECD Centre Registration Certificate** is issued by the **[[Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education (MoPSE)]]** under **Statutory Instrument 106 of 2005**, authorising the operation of Early Childhood Development centres for children aged 0–8 years. The certificate ensures compliance with minimum standards for indoor/outdoor space (2.25m² per child indoor; 5.5m² per child outdoor), teacher-pupil ratios (1:20), sanitation facilities, and qualified staffing. ECD centres must be brought under the supervision of the nearest primary school and operators must register themselves and all teachers with the Ministry.
