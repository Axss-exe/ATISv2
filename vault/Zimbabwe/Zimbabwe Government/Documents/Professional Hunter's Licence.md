## Professional Hunter's Licence

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Zimbabwe Parks and Wildlife Management Authority (ZimParks)]]
**Processing Time**: 30 to 45 working days
**Document Type**: License


## Description
The **Professional Hunter's Licence** is a personal credential issued by the **[[Zimbabwe Parks and Wildlife Management Authority (ZimParks)]]** under the **Parks and Wild Life Act [Chapter 20:14]**, certifying individuals to guide and conduct trophy hunting safaris for foreign and domestic clients on registered safari areas, communal lands, and private game ranches. The licence requires demonstration of extensive knowledge of Zimbabwean wildlife, firearm proficiency, first aid certification, tracking skills, and ethical hunting practices. Professional hunters must operate under the supervision of safari operators, adhere to species-specific quota allocations, and ensure that all hunted animals are properly tagged and documented for CITES compliance where applicable.
