## Shop Licence

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Local Authority]]
**Processing Time**: 14 to 30 working days
**Document Type**: License


## Description
The **Shop Licence** is a municipal permit issued by the **[[Local Authority]]** under the **Shop Licences Act [Chapter 14:17]**, authorising the operation of retail and wholesale businesses from fixed premises within a council's jurisdiction. The licence covers general trading, specific retail categories, and service provision, ensuring compliance with zoning laws, public health standards, and fire safety regulations. Applicants must undergo premises inspection by health inspectors, provide proof of tax compliance, and demonstrate that the business premises meet structural and sanitary requirements before licence issuance.
