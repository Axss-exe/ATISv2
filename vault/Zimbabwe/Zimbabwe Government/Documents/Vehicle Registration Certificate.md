## Vehicle Registration Certificate

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Central Vehicle Registry (CVR)]]
**Processing Time**: 7 to 14 working days
**Document Type**: Certificate


## Description
The **Vehicle Registration Certificate** is issued by the **[[Central Vehicle Registry (CVR)]]** under the **Vehicle Registration and Licensing Act [Chapter 13:05]**, providing official documentation of vehicle ownership, technical specifications, and roadworthiness compliance. The certificate records the vehicle's make, model, engine number, chassis number, colour, and registered owner's details, and is required for legal operation on Zimbabwean roads. Registration involves verification of import documentation (for imported vehicles), roadworthiness inspection, payment of duty and registration fees, and issuance of number plates and a vehicle licence disc.
