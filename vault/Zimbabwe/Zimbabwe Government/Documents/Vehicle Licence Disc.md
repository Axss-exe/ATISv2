## Vehicle Licence Disc

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Zimbabwe National Road Administration (ZINARA)]]; [[Central Vehicle Registry (CVR)]]
**Processing Time**: 1 to 3 working days
**Document Type**: License


## Description
The **Vehicle Licence Disc** is an annual authorisation issued by **[[Zimbabwe National Road Administration (ZINARA)]]** in conjunction with the **[[Central Vehicle Registry (CVR)]]** under the **Vehicle Registration and Licensing Act [Chapter 13:05]** and **Roads Act [Chapter 13:02]**, permitting a motor vehicle to be operated on public roads. The disc certifies that road user fees, third-party insurance, and vehicle licensing fees have been paid for the current year. Renewal requires presentation of the previous licence disc, proof of insurance, and payment of fees calculated based on vehicle type, weight, and engine capacity. Operating without a valid licence disc is a traffic offence subject to fines and vehicle impoundment.
