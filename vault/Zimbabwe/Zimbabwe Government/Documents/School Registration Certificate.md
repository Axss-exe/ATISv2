## School Registration Certificate

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education (MoPSE)]]
**Processing Time**: 3 to 4 months (Head Office target); up to 24 months in practice
**Document Type**: Certificate


## Description
The **School Registration Certificate** is a formal authorisation issued by the **[[Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education (MoPSE)]]** under the **Education Act [Chapter 25:04]**, certifying that a non-government school (including private, church, trust, and local authority schools) meets all statutory requirements for establishment and operation. The certificate confirms compliance with infrastructure standards, teacher qualifications, curriculum requirements, sanitation and water provisions, and pupil-teacher ratios. It must be displayed conspicuously at the school and is subject to renewal and ongoing inspection by Ministry officials.
