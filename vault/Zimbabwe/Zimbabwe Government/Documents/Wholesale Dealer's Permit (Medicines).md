## Wholesale Dealer's Permit (Medicines)

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Medicines Control Authority of Zimbabwe (MCAZ)]]
**Processing Time**: 30 to 45 working days
**Document Type**: Permit


## Description
The **Wholesale Dealer's Permit (Medicines)** is issued by the **[[Medicines Control Authority of Zimbabwe (MCAZ)]]** under the **Medicines and Allied Substances Control Act [Chapter 15:03]**, authorising entities to distribute pharmaceutical products in bulk to retail pharmacies, hospitals, clinics, and licensed medical practitioners. The permit mandates compliance with good distribution practices, including temperature-controlled storage, batch traceability, recall procedures, and record-keeping. Wholesale dealers must employ qualified pharmacists or pharmaceutical technicians, maintain premises that meet MCAZ inspection standards, and secure appointment letters from principal manufacturers for import/export activities.
