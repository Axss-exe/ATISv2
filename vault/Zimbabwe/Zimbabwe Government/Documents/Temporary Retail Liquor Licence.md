## Temporary Retail Liquor Licence

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Liquor Licensing Board]]
**Processing Time**: 14 to 21 working days
**Document Type**: License


## Description
The **Temporary Retail Liquor Licence** is granted by the **[[Liquor Licensing Board]]** under the **Liquor Act [Chapter 14:12]**, authorising the short-term sale of liquor for specific events, functions, or seasonal operations. This licence is typically issued for weddings, festivals, sporting events, or temporary commercial ventures where permanent licensing is impractical. The licence specifies the duration, location, and conditions of sale, and requires the applicant to demonstrate adequate security, public liability coverage, and compliance with local authority health and safety requirements for the event premises.
