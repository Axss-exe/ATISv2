## Research Permit (Wildlife)

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Zimbabwe Parks and Wildlife Management Authority (ZimParks)]]
**Processing Time**: 30 to 60 working days
**Document Type**: Permit


## Description
The **Research Permit (Wildlife)** is issued by the **[[Zimbabwe Parks and Wildlife Management Authority (ZimParks)]]** under the **Parks and Wild Life Act [Chapter 20:14]**, authorising scientists, conservationists, and academic institutions to conduct research on wildlife species, ecosystems, and biodiversity within Zimbabwe's protected areas and communal lands. The permit requires submission of a detailed research proposal, methodology, ethical review, and data sharing agreements. Researchers must comply with animal welfare standards, minimise habitat disturbance, share findings with ZimParks, and ensure that all specimen collection (including genetic samples) is properly documented and permitted.
