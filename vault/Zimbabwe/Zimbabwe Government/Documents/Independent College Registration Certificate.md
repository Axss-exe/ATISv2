## Independent College Registration Certificate

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education (MoPSE)]]; Registrar of Correspondence and Independent Colleges
**Processing Time**: 3 to 4 months (Head Office target); up to 24 months in practice
**Document Type**: Certificate


## Description
The **Independent College Registration Certificate** is issued by the **[[Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education (MoPSE)]]** through the **Registrar of Correspondence and Independent Colleges** under **Part X of the Education Act [Chapter 25:04]**. It authorises private educational institutions to provide face-to-face tuition for students who have completed primary education or attained the age of 16. The registration specifies the scope of programmes offered and requires payment of annual registration fees and guarantee fund contributions to protect student fees in case of college insolvency or failure to meet obligations.
