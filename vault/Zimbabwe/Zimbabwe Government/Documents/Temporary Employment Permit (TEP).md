## Temporary Employment Permit (TEP)

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Department of Immigration]]
**Processing Time**: 30 to 60 working days
**Document Type**: Permit


## Description
The **Temporary Employment Permit (TEP)** is issued by the **[[Department of Immigration]]** under the **Immigration Act [Chapter 4:02]**, authorising foreign nationals to work in Zimbabwe for a specific employer, position, and duration not exceeding five years. The permit requires the employer to demonstrate that the position cannot be filled by a suitably qualified Zimbabwean citizen through a labour market test, and that the foreign worker possesses specialised skills, qualifications, or experience essential to the role. Categories include Class A for skilled professionals, Class B for investors and senior management, Class C for temporary contract workers, Class D for intra-company transfers, and Class E for missionaries and volunteers.
