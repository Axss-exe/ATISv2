## Food Premises Licence

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Local Authority]]
**Processing Time**: 14 to 21 working days
**Document Type**: License


## Description
The **Food Premises Licence** is a health and trading permit issued by the **[[Local Authority]]** under the **Public Health Act [Chapter 15:09]** and **Shop Licences Act [Chapter 14:17]**, authorising the preparation, storage, and sale of food products from commercial premises. The licence mandates compliance with food safety standards including kitchen hygiene, refrigeration requirements, pest control, water supply quality, and staff health certification. Environmental Health Officers conduct regular inspections to verify compliance with Hazard Analysis Critical Control Point (HACCP) principles, and failure to maintain standards can result in immediate closure, fines, or licence revocation.
