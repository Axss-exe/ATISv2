## Occupation Certificate

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Local Authority]]
**Processing Time**: 14 to 30 working days
**Document Type**: Certificate


## Description
The **Occupation Certificate** is issued by the **[[Local Authority]]** under the **Regional Town and Country Planning Act [Chapter 29:12]** and **Urban Councils Act [Chapter 29:15]**, certifying that a completed building is safe for human occupancy and complies with all approved plans, building by-laws, and statutory requirements. The certificate is issued after a series of inspections including foundation verification, drainage installation check, structural completion review, and final building inspection by the council's Building Inspectorate. No building may be legally occupied for residential, commercial, or industrial purposes without a valid Occupation Certificate, and failure to obtain one can result in enforcement action including closure orders and penalties.
