## TVET Institution Registration Certificate

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Higher Education Examinations Council (HEXCO)]]
**Processing Time**: 6 to 12 months
**Document Type**: Registration


## Description
The **TVET Institution Registration Certificate** is issued by the **[[Higher Education Examinations Council (HEXCO)]]** under the **HEXCO Statutory Instrument**, authorising technical and vocational education and training institutions to offer HEXCO-accredited programmes. HEXCO registers public and private TVET institutions, approves examination centres, develops TVET curricula, administers national TVET examinations, and certifies candidates. Registered institutions must meet standards for physical plant, equipment, staff qualifications (lecturers must hold qualifications higher than the level they teach), and quality assurance systems.
