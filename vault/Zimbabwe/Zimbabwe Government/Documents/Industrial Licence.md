## Industrial Licence

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Local Authority]]
**Processing Time**: 30 to 60 working days
**Document Type**: Permit


## Description
The **Industrial Licence** is a municipal permit issued by the **[[Local Authority]]** under the **Urban Councils Act [Chapter 29:15]** and **Factories and Works Act [Chapter 14:08]**, authorising manufacturing, processing, and industrial operations within designated industrial zones. The licence requires compliance with environmental protection standards, noise control regulations, fire safety protocols, and waste management requirements. Applicants must submit factory layout plans, obtain Inspector of Factories approval, demonstrate adequate ventilation and machinery safety, and ensure that industrial effluent disposal meets environmental standards before operations commence.
