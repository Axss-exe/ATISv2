## Security Guard Licence

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Zimbabwe Republic Police (ZRP)]]
**Processing Time**: 14 to 21 working days
**Document Type**: License


## Description
The **Security Guard Licence** is an individual credential issued by the **[[Zimbabwe Republic Police (ZRP)]]** under the **Private Investigators and Security Guards Act [Chapter 27:10]**, authorising a person to work as a security guard for licensed security companies or private employers. The licence requires police clearance verification, minimum physical fitness standards, basic security training, and character references. Guards must wear company-specific uniforms with visible identification, carry only authorised equipment, and adhere to codes of conduct regarding use of force, arrest powers, and property protection. The licence is renewable annually and can be revoked for misconduct or criminal conviction.
