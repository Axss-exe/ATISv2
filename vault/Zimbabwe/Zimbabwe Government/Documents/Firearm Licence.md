## Firearm Licence

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Zimbabwe Republic Police (ZRP)]]
**Processing Time**: 30 to 60 working days
**Document Type**: License


## Description
The **Firearm Licence** is issued by the **[[Zimbabwe Republic Police (ZRP)]]** under the **Firearms Act [Chapter 10:09]**, authorising individuals or entities to possess, carry, and use firearms for legitimate purposes including personal protection, sport shooting, hunting, or security operations. The licence requires thorough background checks, proof of secure storage facilities, competency testing in firearm handling and safety, and justification for the specific type of firearm requested. Categories include personal protection, hunting, sport, and business/security firearms, each with distinct conditions regarding ammunition limits, carrying restrictions, and renewal requirements.
