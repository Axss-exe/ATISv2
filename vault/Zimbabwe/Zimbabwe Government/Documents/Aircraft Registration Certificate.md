## Aircraft Registration Certificate

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Civil Aviation Authority of Zimbabwe (CAAZ)]]
**Processing Time**: 14 to 30 working days
**Document Type**: Registration


## Description
The **Aircraft Registration Certificate** is issued by the **[[Civil Aviation Authority of Zimbabwe (CAAZ)]]** under the **Civil Aviation Act [Chapter 13:16]**, providing official recognition of an aircraft's nationality and ownership within Zimbabwe's civil aircraft register. The certificate assigns a unique registration mark to the aircraft, validates its airworthiness documentation, and establishes the legal framework for its operation within Zimbabwean airspace. Registration requires proof of ownership, de-registration from previous registries (for imported aircraft), compliance with type certification standards, and payment of registration fees based on aircraft weight and category.
