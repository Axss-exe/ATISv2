## Community Broadcasting Service Licence

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Broadcasting Authority of Zimbabwe (BAZ)]]
**Processing Time**: 60 to 90 working days
**Document Type**: License


## Description
The **Community Broadcasting Service Licence** is issued by the **[[Broadcasting Authority of Zimbabwe (BAZ)]]** under the **Broadcasting Services Act [Chapter 12:06]**, permitting non-profit community organisations to operate radio or television services that serve specific geographic or interest-based communities. The licence requires that programming reflect local needs, languages, and cultural expressions, with restrictions on commercial advertising revenue. Holders must demonstrate community ownership, democratic governance structures, and commitment to developmental broadcasting that prioritises education, health, and civic participation over profit.
