## Fire Certificate

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Local Authority Fire Department]]
**Processing Time**: 7 to 14 working days
**Document Type**: Certificate


## Description
The **Fire Certificate** is issued by the **[[Local Authority Fire Department]]** under the **Urban Councils Act [Chapter 29:15]** and **Public Health Act [Chapter 15:09]**, certifying that commercial, industrial, and high-occupancy buildings meet fire prevention, detection, and suppression standards. The certificate requires installation of fire extinguishers, smoke detectors, emergency exit signage, fire hose reels, sprinkler systems (for large buildings), and evacuation plans. Fire officers conduct inspections of electrical systems, flammable material storage, cooking equipment, and building egress routes before issuance. Annual renewal requires re-inspection to ensure continued compliance with evolving fire safety standards and maintenance of fire protection equipment.
