## Veterinary Medicines General Dealer (VMGD) Permit

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Medicines Control Authority of Zimbabwe (MCAZ)]]
**Processing Time**: 14 to 21 working days
**Document Type**: Permit


## Description
The **Veterinary Medicines General Dealer (VMGD) Permit** is granted by the **[[Medicines Control Authority of Zimbabwe (MCAZ)]]** under the **Medicines and Allied Substances Control Act [Chapter 15:03]**, authorising general dealer shops to sell veterinary medicines in the VMGD and Household Remedies (HR Vet) categories. The permit ensures that outlets meet storage, security, and hygiene standards to prevent medicine contamination, deterioration, or theft. Holders may only dispense medicines that do not require veterinary prescription, and must display the permit prominently while maintaining sales records for MCAZ inspection.
