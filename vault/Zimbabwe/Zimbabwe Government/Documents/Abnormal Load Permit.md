## Abnormal Load Permit

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Zimbabwe National Road Administration (ZINARA)]]; [[Vehicle Inspection Department (VID)]]
**Processing Time**: 7 to 14 working days
**Document Type**: Permit


## Description
The **Abnormal Load Permit** is issued by **[[Zimbabwe National Road Administration (ZINARA)]]** with **[[Vehicle Inspection Department (VID)]]** technical clearance under the **Road Traffic Act [Chapter 13:11]** and **Roads Act [Chapter 13:02]**, authorising the transport of loads exceeding standard legal dimensions, weight, or configuration on public roads. The permit requires route surveys to verify bridge load capacities, road width adequacy, and overhead clearance, along with escort vehicle arrangements, travel time restrictions, and public safety notifications. Abnormal loads include heavy machinery, construction equipment, prefabricated structures, and oversized industrial components.
