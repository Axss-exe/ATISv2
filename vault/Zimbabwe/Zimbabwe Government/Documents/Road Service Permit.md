## Road Service Permit

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Local Authority]]; [[Department of Transport]]
**Processing Time**: 21 to 45 working days
**Document Type**: Permit


## Description
The **Road Service Permit** is a route and service authorisation issued by the **[[Local Authority]]** in coordination with the **[[Department of Transport]]** under the **Road Traffic Act [Chapter 13:11]** and **Urban Councils Act [Chapter 29:15]**, permitting public service vehicles to operate on specific routes with defined schedules, stopping points, and passenger capacities. The permit ensures orderly public transport service, prevents route overcrowding, and establishes fare structures that balance operator viability with passenger affordability. Applications require proof of vehicle suitability, driver qualifications, public liability insurance, and compliance with local transport master plans.
