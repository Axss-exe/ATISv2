## Signal Carrier Licence

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Broadcasting Authority of Zimbabwe (BAZ)]]
**Processing Time**: 60 to 90 working days
**Document Type**: License


## Description
The **Signal Carrier Licence** is issued by the **[[Broadcasting Authority of Zimbabwe (BAZ)]]** pursuant to the **Broadcasting Services Act [Chapter 12:06]**, authorising the holder to operate signal transmitting stations for the broadcast of radio and television services. This licence is distinct from broadcasting licences and focuses on the technical infrastructure for signal transmission, ensuring that carriers meet engineering standards, coverage obligations, and interoperability requirements. Only one signal carrier licence may be issued to a non-public broadcaster, and holders must maintain transmission equipment to prescribed technical parameters while preventing interference with other communication services.
