## Tobacco Buyer's Licence (A-Class)

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Tobacco Industry and Marketing Board (TIMB)]]
**Processing Time**: 21 to 30 working days
**Document Type**: License


## Description
The **Tobacco Buyer's Licence (A-Class)** is issued by the **[[Tobacco Industry and Marketing Board (TIMB)]]** under the **Tobacco Marketing and Levy Act**, permitting entities to purchase self-funded unprocessed tobacco at licensed auction floors without prior contractual obligations with growers. The A-Class licence is distinct from contractor licences and is typically used by independent buyers and exporters who source tobacco directly from the auction system. Applicants must demonstrate financial capacity, secure warehouse or processing facilities, and comply with TIMB's grading, pricing, and export documentation requirements.
