## Commercial Broadcasting Service Licence

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Broadcasting Authority of Zimbabwe (BAZ)]]
**Processing Time**: 90 to 120 working days
**Document Type**: License


## Description
The **Commercial Broadcasting Service Licence** is granted by the **[[Broadcasting Authority of Zimbabwe (BAZ)]]** under the **Broadcasting Services Act [Chapter 12:06]**, authorising private entities to operate commercial radio or television stations for profit. The licence obliges holders to comply with programming standards, advertising regulations, local content requirements, and ownership diversity rules. It mandates regular reporting on audience reach, revenue, and content compliance, while subjecting licensees to BAZ monitoring and potential sanctions for violations including suspension or revocation.
