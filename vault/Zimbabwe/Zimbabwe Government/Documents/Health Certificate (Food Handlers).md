## Health Certificate (Food Handlers)

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Local Authority Health Department]]
**Processing Time**: 3 to 7 working days
**Document Type**: Certificate


## Description
The **Health Certificate (Food Handlers)** is a personal health credential issued by the **[[Local Authority Health Department]]** under the **Public Health Act [Chapter 15:09]** and **Food and Food Standards Act [Chapter 15:04]**, certifying that individuals handling food in commercial establishments are free from communicable diseases that could contaminate food products. The certificate requires medical examination including stool sample analysis for typhoid and dysentery pathogens, chest X-ray for tuberculosis screening, and general physical fitness assessment. Food handlers in restaurants, hotels, butcheries, bakeries, and food factories must renew their certificates annually, and employers are legally obligated to ensure that all food handling staff possess valid health certificates.
