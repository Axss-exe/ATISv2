## Tour Operator Licence

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Zimbabwe Tourism Authority (ZTA)]]
**Processing Time**: 21 to 30 working days
**Document Type**: License


## Description
The **Tour Operator Licence** is issued by the **[[Zimbabwe Tourism Authority (ZTA)]]** under the **Tourism Act [Chapter 14:20]**, authorising entities to organise, package, and sell tourism experiences including safaris, cultural tours, adventure activities, and travel itineraries. The licence ensures that operators meet financial capacity requirements, maintain appropriate insurance coverage, employ qualified guides, and adhere to service quality standards that protect tourists and promote Zimbabwe's reputation as a destination. Holders must register all tourism products, maintain booking records, and comply with consumer protection regulations while contributing to the national tourism database.
