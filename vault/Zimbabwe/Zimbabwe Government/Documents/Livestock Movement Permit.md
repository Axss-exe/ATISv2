## Livestock Movement Permit

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Department of Veterinary Services (DVS)]]
**Processing Time**: 3 to 7 working days
**Document Type**: Permit


## Description
The **Livestock Movement Permit** is issued by the **[[Department of Veterinary Services (DVS)]]** under the **Animal Health Act [Chapter 19:01]**, authorising the transportation of cattle, sheep, goats, pigs, and poultry between farms, markets, abattoirs, or across veterinary control zones. The permit is a critical disease control instrument that prevents the spread of notifiable diseases such as foot-and-mouth disease, anthrax, and Newcastle disease by ensuring that only healthy, vaccinated, and properly identified animals move through the national livestock value chain. Each movement requires veterinary inspection, brand or ear tag verification, and compliance with dipping and vaccination schedules.
