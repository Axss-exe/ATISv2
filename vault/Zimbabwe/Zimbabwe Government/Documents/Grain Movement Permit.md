## Grain Movement Permit

**Country**: Zimbabwe
**Issuing Entity**: [[Grain Marketing Board (GMB)]]
**Processing Time**: 3 to 7 working days
**Document Type**: Permit


## Description
The **Grain Movement Permit** is issued by the **[[Grain Marketing Board (GMB)]]** under the **Grain Marketing Act [Chapter 18:14]**, authorising the transportation of specified quantities of grain (maize, wheat, sorghum, millet) between GMB depots, commercial buyers, or across district boundaries. The permit ensures food security monitoring, prevents hoarding and smuggling, and maintains price stability by tracking grain flows within the national supply chain. Permits specify the grain type, quantity, origin, destination, and authorised transporter, and are required for movements exceeding stipulated thresholds, typically from 150 kg to 10 metric tonnes.
