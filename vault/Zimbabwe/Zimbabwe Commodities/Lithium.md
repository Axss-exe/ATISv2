## Lithium

**Category:** 
**Chemical Formula:** Variable (spodumene: LiAl(SiO₃)₂; lepidolite: K(Li,Al)₃(Si,Al)₄O₁₀(F,OH)₂)
**Global Royalty Range:** 3–10%

## Summary

A soft, silvery-white alkali metal, the lightest solid element. Essential for energy storage. Does not occur free in nature.


## Geological Occurrence

- 1. Hard-rock pegmatites (spodumene, petalite, lepidolite) — Australia, Canada, Zimbabwe, DRC, Namibia
2. Brine evaporation ponds — Chile, Argentina, Bolivia (Salar de Atacama, Uyuni)
3. Clay deposits — Nevada, Serbia
4. Oilfield brines — Arkansas, North Dakota


## Major End Products

- 1. Lithium-ion EV battery cathodes (NMC, LFP)
2. Grid-scale energy storage systems
3. Ceramic & glass fluxes (lithium carbonate)
4. High-temperature lubricating greases (lithium hydroxide)
5. Aluminum smelting bath additives


## Standard Processing Workflow

- 1. Mining (hard rock) or brine extraction
2. Crushing & grinding (hard rock)
3. Dense media separation or froth flotation (spodumene)
4. Acid roasting (α-spodumene + H₂SO₄ at ~1,050°C)
5. Water leaching of lithium sulphate
6. Impurity removal (Mg, Ca, Fe precipitation)
7. Lithium carbonate (Li₂CO₃) or hydroxide (LiOH) precipitation
8. Crystallization, drying, & packaging


## Common Mining Methods

- • Open-pit bench mining (hard rock)
• Underground room and pillar (deep pegmatites)
• Brine extraction (pumping + solar evaporation ponds)
• In-situ leaching (clay deposits, experimental)
