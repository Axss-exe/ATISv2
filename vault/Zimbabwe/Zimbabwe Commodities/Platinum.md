## Platinum

**Category:** 
**Chemical Formula:** Pt (PGM suite: Pt, Pd, Rh, Ru, Ir, Os)
**Global Royalty Range:** 2–10%

## Summary

A dense, silvery-white platinum group metal (PGM). Highly unreactive, resistant to corrosion, excellent catalyst. Melting point 1,768°C. Found with palladium, rhodium, ruthenium, iridium, osmium.


## Geological Occurrence

- 1. Layered mafic intrusions — Bushveld (South Africa), Great Dyke (Zimbabwe), Stillwater (USA)
2. Norite / pyroxenite reefs — stratiform PGM layers
3. Dunite pipes — Merensky Reef, UG2, Platreef
4. Alluvial / placer deposits — Russia (Kondyor), Colombia
5. Nickel-copper sulphide deposits — Sudbury, Norilsk (by-product PGM)


## Major End Products

- 1. Automotive catalytic converters (primary demand, ~40%)
2. Fine jewelry, investment products & watch cases
3. Chemical process catalysts (nitric acid, silicone, petroleum)
4. Medical stents, pacemakers, dental alloys & chemotherapy drugs
5. Petroleum refining catalysts & glass manufacturing


## Standard Processing Workflow

- 1. Mechanized underground mining of PGM ore
2. Crushing & milling (SAG/ball mills)
3. Froth flotation to produce PGM concentrate (0.5–2% PGM)
4. Smelting to matte (electric furnace + Peirce-Smith converter)
5. Base Metals Refinery (BMR): dissolve matte in HCl/Cl₂, separate Ni, Cu, Co
6. Precious Metals Refinery (PMR): solvent extraction of individual PGMs
7. Precipitation of PGM salts (ammonium hexachloroplatinate)
8. Calcination to sponge (Pt, Pd, Rh)
9. Arc melting to ingots or atomization to grain


## Common Mining Methods

- • Mechanized underground room and pillar (Bushveld, Great Dyke standard)
• Breast stoping & bord-and-pillar
• Decline shaft access (ramp decline)
• Trackless mechanized mining (load-haul-dump)
• Limited open-pit (shallow oxide caps, Platreef)
