## Copper

**Category:** 
**Chemical Formula:** Cu
**Global Royalty Range:** 2–6%

## Summary

A reddish, malleable base metal with exceptional thermal and electrical conductivity. Third-most consumed industrial metal after iron and aluminum.


## Geological Occurrence

- 1. Porphyry deposits — Chile, Peru, Arizona, Mongolia, Zambia, DRC
2. Sediment-hosted stratiform — Zambian Copperbelt, DRC Katanga
3. Volcanogenic massive sulphide (VMS) — Iberian Pyrite Belt, Cyprus, Tasmania
4. Oxide / supergene blankets — above sulphide deposits
5. Skarn deposits — Russia, Philippines


## Major End Products

- 1. Electrical wire, cable & busbars
2. Plumbing, HVAC tubing & fittings
3. EV motor windings, inverters & charging infrastructure
4. Renewable energy (solar farms, wind turbines, grid storage)
5. Electronic circuit boards, semiconductors & heat exchangers


## Standard Processing Workflow

- 1. Mining (oxide or sulphide ore)
2. Crushing & grinding
3. Froth flotation (sulphides) or acid leaching (oxide ores, SX-EW)
4. Concentrate smelting in reverberatory or flash furnace
5. Converting to blister copper (98–99%)
6. Fire refining to anode copper
7. Electrolytic refining to cathodes (99.99% Cu)


## Common Mining Methods

- • Open-pit (porphyry, bulk deposits)
• Underground block caving (deep porphyry)
• Sub-level stoping (narrow veins)
• Room and pillar
• In-situ leaching (oxide deposits)
• Solution mining
