# TelOne (State-Owned Fibre & Telecom)

**Infrastructure Type:**  Telecommunications Network
**Operational Status:** `operational`
**Operated By**: [[TelOne]]
**Value Is**: The state-owned telecommunications provider operating Zimbabwe's national fibre backbone and international gateways. Its existence ensures government and SOE communications, mining licence application portals, and ZIMRA customs systems remain connected. Without TelOne, state regulatory functions (licensing, taxation, customs) would face system outages, and rural mining communities would have no basic telecom access. The network suffers from underinvestment, aging copper last-mile infrastructure, and competition from private operators.


## Summary

The state-owned telecommunications provider operating Zimbabwe's national fibre backbone and international gateways. Its existence ensures government and SOE communications, mining licence application portals, and ZIMRA customs systems remain connected. Without TelOne, state regulatory functions (licensing, taxation, customs) would face system outages, and rural mining communities would have no basic telecom access. The network suffers from underinvestment, aging copper last-mile infrastructure, and competition from private operators.

#### Technical Notes: 
National fibre backbone; ADSL, fibre-to-home, wholesale
