# Port of Beira (Mozambique — Primary Zimbabwe Access)

**Infrastructure Type:**  Seaport
**Operational Status:** `operational`
**Operated By**: [[Cornelder de Mozambique]]
**Value Is**: The nearest deep-water seaport to Zimbabwe (600 km via Machipanda), operated under concession by Cornelder de Mozambique. Its existence is Zimbabwe's most competitive maritime access for bulk chrome and nickel exports to China and Europe. Without Beira, all bulk mineral exports must transit Durban at 2x the distance and cost, eroding Zimbabwe's already thin margins. The port suffered severe cyclone damage in 2019 and 2021, and channel siltation requires continuous dredging. Rail link condition from Machipanda is a major constraint.


## Summary

The nearest deep-water seaport to Zimbabwe (600 km via Machipanda), operated under concession by Cornelder de Mozambique. Its existence is Zimbabwe's most competitive maritime access for bulk chrome and nickel exports to China and Europe. Without Beira, all bulk mineral exports must transit Durban at 2x the distance and cost, eroding Zimbabwe's already thin margins. The port suffered severe cyclone damage in 2019 and 2021, and channel siltation requires continuous dredging. Rail link condition from Machipanda is a major constraint.

#### Technical Notes: 
17 million tonnes/year capacity; draft 8–12 m
