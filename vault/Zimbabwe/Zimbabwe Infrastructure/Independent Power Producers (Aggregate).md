# Independent Power Producers (Aggregate)

**Infrastructure Type:**  Power Generation Network
**Operational Status:** `operational`
**Operated By**: Multiple private operators
**Value Is**: Distributed renewable and bagasse generation that represents Zimbabwe's only significant non-ZPC power supply. Their existence provides off-grid and grid-supplementary power for remote mining operations, reducing dependence on the failing national grid. Without IPPs, greenfield lithium and gold projects in rural areas would require prohibitively expensive diesel self-generation. However, aggregate output is insufficient for base-load smelting, and grid integration remains problematic due to ZETDC's weak distribution network.


## Summary

Distributed renewable and bagasse generation that represents Zimbabwe's only significant non-ZPC power supply. Their existence provides off-grid and grid-supplementary power for remote mining operations, reducing dependence on the failing national grid. Without IPPs, greenfield lithium and gold projects in rural areas would require prohibitively expensive diesel self-generation. However, aggregate output is insufficient for base-load smelting, and grid integration remains problematic due to ZETDC's weak distribution network.

#### Technical Notes: 
~140 MW aggregate (solar, bagasse, mini-hydro)
