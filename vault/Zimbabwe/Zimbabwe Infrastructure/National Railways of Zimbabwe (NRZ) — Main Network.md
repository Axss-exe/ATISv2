# National Railways of Zimbabwe (NRZ) — Main Network

**Infrastructure Type:**  Railway Network
**Operational Status:** `partially_operational`
**Operated By**: [[National Railways of Zimbabwe (NRZ)]]
**Value Is**: The national rail backbone, founded 1893, linking Zimbabwe to regional corridors (South Africa, Zambia, Mozambique, Botswana). Its existence is the only viable bulk transport mode for heavy mining commodities (coal, chrome, nickel, PGM concentrate). Without NRZ, mines must truck bulk ore to ports at 3–5x the cost, eroding margins and accelerating road deterioration. The network's collapse from 18 million tonnes (1998) to ~2 million tonnes (2010) forced a modal shift to trucking that has crippled Zimbabwe's export competitiveness. Passenger services were suspended in 2020.


## Summary

The national rail backbone, founded 1893, linking Zimbabwe to regional corridors (South Africa, Zambia, Mozambique, Botswana). Its existence is the only viable bulk transport mode for heavy mining commodities (coal, chrome, nickel, PGM concentrate). Without NRZ, mines must truck bulk ore to ports at 3–5x the cost, eroding margins and accelerating road deterioration. The network's collapse from 18 million tonnes (1998) to ~2 million tonnes (2010) forced a modal shift to trucking that has crippled Zimbabwe's export competitiveness. Passenger services were suspended in 2020.

#### Technical Notes: 
4,225 km network; freight capacity severely constrained
