# Bulawayo Bulk Supply Point

**Infrastructure Type:**  Electrical Substation
**Operational Status:** `operational`
**Operated By**: [[Zimbabwe Electricity Transmission and Distribution Company (ZETDC)]]
**Value Is**: The primary grid node for Bulawayo and southern Zimbabwe. Its existence enables power delivery to the Unki platinum mine, chrome smelters, and the Beitbridge border logistics hub. Without it, the southern mining belt would be electrically isolated from Kariba and Hwange, forcing reliance on expensive diesel generation. The substation's aging switchgear and insufficient capacity for smelter expansion are bottlenecks for PGM sector growth.


## Summary

The primary grid node for Bulawayo and southern Zimbabwe. Its existence enables power delivery to the Unki platinum mine, chrome smelters, and the Beitbridge border logistics hub. Without it, the southern mining belt would be electrically isolated from Kariba and Hwange, forcing reliance on expensive diesel generation. The substation's aging switchgear and insufficient capacity for smelter expansion are bottlenecks for PGM sector growth.

#### Technical Notes:
