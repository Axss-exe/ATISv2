# A9 Highway (Mutare–Masvingo–Bulawayo)

**Infrastructure Type:**  Highway
**Operational Status:** `partially_operational`
**Operated By**: [[Zimbabwe National Road Administration (ZINARA)]]
**Value Is**: A strategic east-west corridor linking the Beira port corridor to western mining regions. Its existence enables chrome from the Midlands to reach Beira port without transiting Harare, and provides an alternative cross-country emergency route. Without it, all Beira-bound traffic must use the longer Harare–Chirundu–Lilongwe route or the congested A4. Cyclone Idai (2019) destroyed multiple bridges, and reconstruction remains incomplete.


## Summary

A strategic east-west corridor linking the Beira port corridor to western mining regions. Its existence enables chrome from the Midlands to reach Beira port without transiting Harare, and provides an alternative cross-country emergency route. Without it, all Beira-bound traffic must use the longer Harare–Chirundu–Lilongwe route or the congested A4. Cyclone Idai (2019) destroyed multiple bridges, and reconstruction remains incomplete.
