# Kazungula Border Post

**Infrastructure Type:**  Border Crossing
**Operational Status:** `under_construction / planned`
**Operated By**: [[Zimbabwe Revenue Authority (ZRA)]]
**Value Is**: A new border post at the Zambezi River crossing where four countries meet, with a bridge completed in 2021. Its existence will provide an alternative to Beitbridge for DRC and Zambian mineral transit to South African and Namibian ports. Without Zimbabwe's completed facilities, the country will miss a strategic opportunity to capture transit revenue and decongest Beitbridge. Zimbabwe's side lags significantly behind Zambia and Botswana in construction progress.


## Summary

A new border post at the Zambezi River crossing where four countries meet, with a bridge completed in 2021. Its existence will provide an alternative to Beitbridge for DRC and Zambian mineral transit to South African and Namibian ports. Without Zimbabwe's completed facilities, the country will miss a strategic opportunity to capture transit revenue and decongest Beitbridge. Zimbabwe's side lags significantly behind Zambia and Botswana in construction progress.

#### Technical Notes: 
Quadripartite OSBP (Zambia, Botswana, Zimbabwe, Namibia)
