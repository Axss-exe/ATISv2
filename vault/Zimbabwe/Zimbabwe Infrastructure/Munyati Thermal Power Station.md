# Munyati Thermal Power Station

**Infrastructure Type:**  Power Station
**Operational Status:** `dormant / under_rehabilitation`
**Operated By**: [[Zimbabwe Power Company (ZPC)]]
**Value Is**: A small thermal in the Midlands commissioned 1946–1957. Its existence historically supported the Kwekwe-Gweru mining corridor. Without it, the Midlands chrome and nickel belt faces power deficits during peak demand, forcing load shedding on mining operations. The plant's location near the rail line and coal fields makes it theoretically viable for biomass co-firing or gas conversion, but capital constraints have prevented any redevelopment.


## Summary

A small thermal in the Midlands commissioned 1946–1957. Its existence historically supported the Kwekwe-Gweru mining corridor. Without it, the Midlands chrome and nickel belt faces power deficits during peak demand, forcing load shedding on mining operations. The plant's location near the rail line and coal fields makes it theoretically viable for biomass co-firing or gas conversion, but capital constraints have prevented any redevelopment.

#### Technical Notes: 
100 MW installed; ~0 MW dependable
