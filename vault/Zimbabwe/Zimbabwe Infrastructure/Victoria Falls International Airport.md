# Victoria Falls International Airport

**Infrastructure Type:**  Airport
**Operational Status:** `operational`
**Operated By**: [[Airports Company of Zimbabwe (ACZ)]]
**Value Is**: A regional tourism and logistics hub with the longest runway in Zimbabwe (4,000 m), upgraded in 2016 to handle wide-body aircraft. Its existence provides direct international access to western Zimbabwe's mining regions (Hwange coal, RHA tungsten) and the Victoria Falls tourism economy. Without it, western mining operations would rely on 12-hour road transfers from Harare or Bulawayo, delaying critical personnel and equipment movements. The airport's cargo facilities remain underdeveloped, limiting its utility for mining logistics.


## Summary

A regional tourism and logistics hub with the longest runway in Zimbabwe (4,000 m), upgraded in 2016 to handle wide-body aircraft. Its existence provides direct international access to western Zimbabwe's mining regions (Hwange coal, RHA tungsten) and the Victoria Falls tourism economy. Without it, western mining operations would rely on 12-hour road transfers from Harare or Bulawayo, delaying critical personnel and equipment movements. The airport's cargo facilities remain underdeveloped, limiting its utility for mining logistics.

#### Technical Notes: 
4,000 m runway (upgraded 2016); capable of wide-body aircraft
