# A1 Highway (Harare–Chirundu)

**Infrastructure Type:**  Highway
**Operational Status:** `under_rehabilitation`
**Operated By**: [[Zimbabwe National Road Administration (ZINARA)]]
**Value Is**: The principal highway to Zambia and the DRC Copperbelt, part of the North-South Corridor. Its existence enables copper concentrate transit from [[Zambia]] to [[South African]] ports, cobalt logistics, and mining equipment delivery to the Copperbelt. Without it, Zambia's mining sector would lose its primary southern export route, and Zimbabwe would forfeit significant transit revenue. The escarpment section is prone to landslides and seasonal flooding, causing multi-day closures that disrupt just-in-time mining supply chains.


## Summary

The principal highway to Zambia and the DRC Copperbelt, part of the North-South Corridor. Its existence enables copper concentrate transit from Zambia to South African ports, cobalt logistics, and mining equipment delivery to the Copperbelt. Without it, Zambia's mining sector would lose its primary southern export route, and Zimbabwe would forfeit significant transit revenue. The escarpment section is prone to landslides and seasonal flooding, causing multi-day closures that disrupt just-in-time mining supply chains.

#### Technical Notes: 
~350 km; links to Zambia
