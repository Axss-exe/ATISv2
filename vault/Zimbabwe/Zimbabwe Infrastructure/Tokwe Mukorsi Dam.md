# Tokwe Mukorsi Dam

**Infrastructure Type:**  Dam
**Operational Status:** `operational`
**Operated By**: [[Zimbabwe]]
**Value Is**: Zimbabwe's largest inland reservoir by volume, completed in 2016 after flooding controversies and cost overruns. Its existence provides irrigation water for sugar estates, flood control for the Tokwe River basin, and potential future mini-hydro capacity. Without it, Masvingo province's agricultural output would be halved, and the Lowveld's sugar-cogeneration power potential would be constrained. For mining, it represents a strategic water reserve for future operations in the southeastern region and a case study in Chinese infrastructure finance (Export-Import Bank of China).


## Summary

Zimbabwe's largest inland reservoir by volume, completed in 2016 after flooding controversies and cost overruns. Its existence provides irrigation water for sugar estates, flood control for the Tokwe River basin, and potential future mini-hydro capacity. Without it, Masvingo province's agricultural output would be halved, and the Lowveld's sugar-cogeneration power potential would be constrained. For mining, it represents a strategic water reserve for future operations in the southeastern region and a case study in Chinese infrastructure finance (Export-Import Bank of China).
