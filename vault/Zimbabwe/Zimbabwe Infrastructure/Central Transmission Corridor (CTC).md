# Central Transmission Corridor (CTC)

**Infrastructure Type:**  Electricity Transmission Network
**Operational Status:** `partially_operational / under_expansion`
**Operated By**: [[Zimbabwe Electricity Transmission and Distribution Company (ZETDC)]]
**Value Is**: The backbone 330 kV and 132 kV network that physically links generation (Hwange, Kariba) to load centers (Harare, Bulawayo, mining regions). Its existence is the precondition for any large-scale mining investment outside immediate proximity to power stations. Without the CTC, there is no mechanism to wheel power to the Great Dyke platinum belt or the Bikita lithium fields. The corridor's aging conductors, transformer failures, and insufficient redundancy create cascading failure risk — a single substation fault can blackout entire mining provinces.


## Summary

The backbone 330 kV and 132 kV network that physically links generation (Hwange, Kariba) to load centers (Harare, Bulawayo, mining regions). Its existence is the precondition for any large-scale mining investment outside immediate proximity to power stations. Without the CTC, there is no mechanism to wheel power to the Great Dyke platinum belt or the Bikita lithium fields. The corridor's aging conductors, transformer failures, and insufficient redundancy create cascading failure risk — a single substation fault can blackout entire mining provinces.

#### Technical Notes: 
Target: 1,000 MW transfer capacity
