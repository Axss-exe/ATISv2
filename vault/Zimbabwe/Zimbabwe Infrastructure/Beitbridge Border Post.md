# Beitbridge Border Post

**Infrastructure Type:**  Border Crossing
**Operational Status:** `operational / under_upgrade`
**Operated By**: [[Zimbabwe Revenue Authority (ZIMRA)]]
**Value Is**: The principal border crossing with South Africa on the Limpopo River, handling 40%+ of Zimbabwe's international trade. Its existence is the chokepoint for all chrome, platinum, and coal exports to Richards Bay and Durban, and for mining equipment, fuel, and spare parts imports. Without efficient Beitbridge operations, mining supply chains face 2–5 day delays, diesel shortages, and production halts. The border's chronic congestion (2+ day truck queues), scanner shortages, and staff deficits (~300 vs. 500 needed) represent a systemic national risk. A PPP upgrade was approved in 2009 but stalled for 15 years; new concession arrangements are now under negotiation.


## Summary

The principal border crossing with South Africa on the Limpopo River, handling 40%+ of Zimbabwe's international trade. Its existence is the chokepoint for all chrome, platinum, and coal exports to Richards Bay and Durban, and for mining equipment, fuel, and spare parts imports. Without efficient Beitbridge operations, mining supply chains face 2–5 day delays, diesel shortages, and production halts. The border's chronic congestion (2+ day truck queues), scanner shortages, and staff deficits (~300 vs. 500 needed) represent a systemic national risk. A PPP upgrade was approved in 2009 but stalled for 15 years; new concession arrangements are now under negotiation.

#### Technical Notes: 
Busiest in Southern Africa; ~500 trucks/day; 24-hour operation
