# Mvuma–Harare Water Pipeline (planned)

**Infrastructure Type:**  Water Pipeline
**Operational Status:** `planned`
**Operated By**: [[Zimbabwe National Water Authority (ZINWA)]]
**Value Is**: A proposed pipeline to augment Harare's water supply from the Mvuma area, potentially extending to Mashonaland West's lithium mining belt. Its existence would relieve chronic water shortages in Harare and provide industrial water for lithium processing plants that require 500,000+ m³/year each. Without it, lithium developers must build private water treatment plants or deep boreholes, adding \$10–30M to project capex. The project has been in planning for over a decade with no construction timeline.


## Summary

A proposed pipeline to augment Harare's water supply from the Mvuma area, potentially extending to Mashonaland West's lithium mining belt. Its existence would relieve chronic water shortages in Harare and provide industrial water for lithium processing plants that require 500,000+ m³/year each. Without it, lithium developers must build private water treatment plants or deep boreholes, adding \$10–30M to project capex. The project has been in planning for over a decade with no construction timeline.

Proposed; part of national water master plan
