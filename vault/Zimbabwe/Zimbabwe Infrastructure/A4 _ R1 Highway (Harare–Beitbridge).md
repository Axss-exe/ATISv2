# A4 / R1 Highway (Harare–Beitbridge)

**Infrastructure Type:**  Highway
**Operational Status:** `under_rehabilitation`
**Operated By**: [[Zimbabwe National Road Administration (ZINARA)]]
**Value Is**: The principal highway to South Africa, carrying 40%+ of Zimbabwe's international trade by road. Its existence is the lifeline for chrome, platinum, and coal exports to Richards Bay and Durban, and for mining equipment, fuel, and spare parts imports. Without a functional A4, the mining sector would face 2–3 day delivery delays, diesel shortages, and equipment downtime that halts production. The road's chronic potholes, single-carriageway sections, and bridge failures are a national emergency. Dualization has been discussed since 2009 but only partially implemented.


## Summary

The principal highway to South Africa, carrying 40%+ of Zimbabwe's international trade by road. Its existence is the lifeline for chrome, platinum, and coal exports to Richards Bay and Durban, and for mining equipment, fuel, and spare parts imports. Without a functional A4, the mining sector would face 2–3 day delivery delays, diesel shortages, and equipment downtime that halts production. The road's chronic potholes, single-carriageway sections, and bridge failures are a national emergency. Dualization has been discussed since 2009 but only partially implemented.

571 km; primary North-South Corridor
