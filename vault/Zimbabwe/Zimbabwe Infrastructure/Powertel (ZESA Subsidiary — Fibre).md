# Powertel (ZESA Subsidiary — Fibre)

**Infrastructure Type:**  Telecommunications Network
**Operational Status:** `operational`
**Operated By**: [[Powertel]]
**Value Is**: A ZESA subsidiary leveraging 330 kV and 132 kV transmission line corridors for fibre deployment, focused on utility and corporate clients. Its existence provides smart grid communications for power infrastructure monitoring and backup corporate connectivity for mines. Without Powertel, ZESA would lack real-time grid monitoring, and mine substations would be blind to power quality issues. The network is limited to transmission corridors, leaving large rural mining areas unserved.


## Summary

A ZESA subsidiary leveraging 330 kV and 132 kV transmission line corridors for fibre deployment, focused on utility and corporate clients. Its existence provides smart grid communications for power infrastructure monitoring and backup corporate connectivity for mines. Without Powertel, ZESA would lack real-time grid monitoring, and mine substations would be blind to power quality issues. The network is limited to transmission corridors, leaving large rural mining areas unserved.

#### Technical Notes: 
Fibre along power transmission corridors; utility-focused
