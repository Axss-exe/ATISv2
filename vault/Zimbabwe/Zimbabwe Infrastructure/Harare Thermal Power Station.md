## Summary

The oldest of the small thermals, commissioned 1942–1957. Its existence once provided dedicated power to Mashonaland's mining and agricultural processing industries. Without it, northern lithium and gold operations depend entirely on the Central Transmission Corridor, creating single-point-of-failure risk. The plant's dormancy forces mines to invest in expensive diesel backup or solar hybrid systems, eroding project economics. Conversion to an Independent Power Producer (IPP) model has been discussed since 2015 but never executed.

#### Technical Notes: 
30–120 MW (varies by source); ~0 MW dependable
